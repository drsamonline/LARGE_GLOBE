The Kerala School

A school of mathematicians in Kerala who derived infinite series for trigonometric functions centuries before Europe.

The mathematics

The Kerala school, associated with Madhava and later Nilakantha, developed infinite series expansions for sine, cosine and arctangent, with remainder terms, between the fourteenth and sixteenth centuries. The results are documented in Sanskrit texts.

Maps, diagrams and images

The question

Whether these results reached Europe before Newton and Leibniz is argued; no documentary route has been established, and most historians treat the developments as independent.

What is established

Documented: the series and their dates.

Documented: the texts.

Unestablished: transmission to Europe.

The label: documented.

Why it is in the archive

Because it is a genuine priority claim, made carefully, in a field where such claims are usually inflated.
