Unfavorable Semicircle

A YouTube channel that uploaded tens of thousands of silent, cryptic videos. It stopped, and nobody has explained it.

The channel

From 2015 a YouTube channel called Unfavorable Semicircle uploaded thousands of videos with cryptic titles, silent audio and images that were often nearly blank. The uploads stopped in 2016, and a community archived and analysed every file.

Maps, diagrams and images

The explanations

Proposals include an automated system generating content, an art project, a puzzle and a hoax. Analysis found patterns in the file sizes and titles but no message, and no creator has come forward with a verified account.

The archive files it as live debate: the material is preserved and the meaning is missing.

Where it stands

Documented: the videos and their archive.

Documented: the community's analysis.

Not established: the creator or purpose.

Plausible: an automated or artistic project.

The honest label: live debate.

Why it is in the archive

Because it is a case where the data survived and the explanation did not.
