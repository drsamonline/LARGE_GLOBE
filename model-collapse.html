Model Collapse

Training on model output is said to degrade models. The phenomenon is documented in some settings; the generality is argued.

The claim

A 2023 paper argued that training generative models on their own output leads to 'model collapse' — a progressive loss of diversity and fidelity. The effect was demonstrated in text and image models.

Maps, diagrams and images

The qualifications

Later work found that collapse depends on the ratio of synthetic to real data, and that accumulating real data or retaining original samples prevents it. The phenomenon is documented; the claim that it is inevitable is not.

Where it stands

Documented: the effect in some settings.

Documented: the conditions.

Contested: inevitability.

The honest label: live debate.

Why it is in the archive
