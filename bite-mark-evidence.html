Bite Mark Evidence

Dentists testified that a wound matched a suspect's teeth. The science was never validated, and people were convicted.

The practice

Forensic odontologists compared bite marks on skin with dental casts of suspects, and testified to matches in criminal trials from the 1970s. Convictions followed, some in capital cases.

Maps, diagrams and images

The evidence

The 2009 National Academy of Sciences report found no scientific basis for bite-mark comparison, and subsequent reviews documented wrongful convictions. Skin is elastic, wounds distort, and different dentists disagree. The practice has been abandoned in several jurisdictions.

What is established

Documented: the practice and the convictions.

Documented: the NAS finding.

Not validated: the comparison.

The label: debunked.

Why it is in the archive

Because it is testimony presented as science, and the archive can show the difference.
