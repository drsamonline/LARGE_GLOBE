Tachyons

Particles that always travel faster than light. They are mathematically possible and physically absent.

The idea

Tachyons are hypothetical particles with imaginary mass that always travel faster than light. They appear in some solutions of relativity and in string theory, and the term is used for unstable fields in cosmology.

Maps, diagrams and images

The problems

A tachyon that carries energy and signals would violate causality, allowing effects before causes in some frames. No tachyon has been detected, and the 'tachyon' of string theory is an instability of the vacuum rather than a particle that travels. Searches at colliders have found nothing.

The archive files it as fringe because the mathematical object is real and the particle is not.

Where it stands

Documented: the mathematical solutions.

Not observed: any tachyon.

Problematic: causality.

Different: the string-theory usage.

The honest label: fringe.

Why it is in the archive

Because it is the particle everyone has heard of and nobody has seen.
