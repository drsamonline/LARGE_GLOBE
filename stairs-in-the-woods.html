Stairs in the Woods

A creepypasta about flights of stairs found in forests, leading nowhere. It began as an imageboard thread and became a genre fixture.

The story

The trope began with an imageboard post in which hikers describe finding flights of stairs in the woods, with no building attached, and report that the stairs cannot be climbed without something going wrong. Later stories added rules and consequences.

Maps, diagrams and images

The status

The stories are fiction, written for horror communities. Photographs of forest stairs exist because stairs exist in forests — access paths, hunting platforms, ruins — which is what makes the fiction effective.

The archive files it as fiction and notes the mechanism: a real, ordinary object placed out of context.

What is established

Documented: the stories and their origin.

Documented: the real objects that resemble them.

Not established: any anomaly.

The mechanism: context removal.

The label: fiction.

Why it is in the archive

Because it shows how much of a scare comes from a real photograph and a missing sentence.
