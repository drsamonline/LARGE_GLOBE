The Comic Book Panic

Comic books were blamed for juvenile delinquency, and a Senate subcommittee and an industry code followed.

The panic

In the late 1940s and early 1950s, psychiatrist Fredric Wertham's book argued that comics caused delinquency and sexual deviance. A Senate subcommittee investigated, and the industry adopted a Comics Code that removed whole genres.

Maps, diagrams and images

The evidence

The research behind the panic was criticised for method, and later work found no causal link. The episode is documented in cultural history and is a standard case of a moral panic shaping an industry.
