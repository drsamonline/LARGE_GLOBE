The Jantar Mantar

Stone instruments built to measure the sky to a precision that still works.

The instruments

Maharaja Jai Singh II built observatories at Jaipur, Delhi, Ujjain, Varanasi and Mathura in the early eighteenth century, using masonry instruments of very large size to improve accuracy by reducing angular error.

Maps, diagrams and images

The achievement

The instruments are documented and several still function; the Samrat Yantra at Jaipur is a sundial accurate to about two seconds. The observatory is a UNESCO World Heritage Site, and its design is studied as an engineering achievement rather than a curiosity.

Why it is in the archive

Because it is the case where the monument is the instrument, and it still works.
