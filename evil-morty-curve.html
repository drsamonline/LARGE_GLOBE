Evil Morty's Curve

The theory that the show's plot is driven by a statistical curve of Mortys. It is a joke about narrative and it is treated as one.

The theory

In Rick and Morty, a character refers to a curve describing the distribution of Mortys across realities, used to explain why Rick is always paired with a Morty. The theory treats the curve as a real in-universe law governing the plot.

Maps, diagrams and images

The problems

The curve is a narrative device introduced for a joke and a plot twist, and the show's creators treat its canon loosely. The theory's supporters build a cosmology on it, which the show neither confirms nor needs.

The archive files it as fiction and notes the mechanism: a joke's internal logic treated as worldbuilding.

What is established

Documented: the curve in dialogue.

Documented: the theory.

Not established: any in-universe law.

The mechanism: joke logic as canon.

The label: fiction.

Why it is in the archive

Because it shows how a single line of dialogue can generate a cosmology.
