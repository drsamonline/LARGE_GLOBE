Aspartame and Cancer

A sweetener classified as possibly carcinogenic, and the dose that matters.

The classification

In 2023 the International Agency for Research on Cancer classified aspartame as possibly carcinogenic to humans (group 2B), based on limited evidence in humans. A separate assessment by the food-safety body kept the acceptable daily intake unchanged.

Maps, diagrams and images

The reading

Group 2B means the evidence is limited, and it sits alongside aloe vera and pickled vegetables. The assessments found no increased risk at ordinary consumption levels, and the classification is a hazard statement rather than a dietary instruction.

Where it stands

Documented: the classification.

Documented: the intake limit unchanged.

Limited: the human evidence.

Not raised: the risk at normal intake.

The honest label: live debate.

Why it is in the archive

Because the difference between hazard and risk is the most misreported thing in food science.
