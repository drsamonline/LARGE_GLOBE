Benchmark Contamination

Test sets end up in training data, so the scores measure memory. The problem is documented and hard to fix.

The problem

Large models are trained on web text, and public benchmarks are on the web. Studies have found that models can reproduce test items verbatim, and that scores on contaminated benchmarks overstate capability.

Maps, diagrams and images

The responses

Documented responses include private held-out sets, canary strings, and dynamic benchmarks generated fresh. None is complete, and contamination detection is itself an active research problem.
