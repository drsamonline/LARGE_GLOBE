Cow Urine as Medicine

A traditional preparation promoted as a cure, and tested. The tests do not support the claims.

The claim

Cow urine is used in some traditional preparations and has been promoted by practitioners and politicians as a treatment for conditions including cancer and diabetes, and as a disinfectant.

Maps, diagrams and images

The evidence

No clinical trial has shown efficacy for these claims, and the preparation is not sterile. Regulators have taken action against specific products, and the evidence base is documented as absent rather than disputed.

What is established

Documented: the practice.

Not established: any efficacy.

Documented: regulatory action.

The label: debunked.

Why it is in the archive

Because tradition is not evidence, and this claim is made with official support.
