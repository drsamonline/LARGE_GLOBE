Sai Baba's Miracles

A figure credited with materialising objects and knowing the future. The claims are testimony; the demonstrations are not testable.

The claims

Sathya Sai Baba was credited by followers with materialising objects such as sacred ash, producing food, and knowing events at a distance. The accounts are numerous and are documented in devotional and journalistic literature.

Maps, diagrams and images

The evidence

No materialisation has been demonstrated under controlled conditions, and magicians have published explanations of the methods used in such performances. The claims are testimony, which is a form of evidence, and it is not the kind that settles a physical question.

Where it stands

Documented: the claims and testimony.

Not demonstrated: under controls.

Explained: as conjuring by critics.

The honest label: fringe.

Why it is in the archive

Because millions of people witnessed these claims, which makes them worth recording carefully and neutrally.
