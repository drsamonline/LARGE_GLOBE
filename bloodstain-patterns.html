Bloodstain Pattern Analysis

The shapes of bloodstains are used to reconstruct events. The physics is real; the inferences are argued.

The method

Bloodstain pattern analysis uses the size, shape and distribution of stains to infer the events that produced them — impact, cast-off, arterial spatter, transfer. The underlying fluid dynamics is documented.

Maps, diagrams and images

The problems

The inference from pattern to event involves judgement, and studies have found wide disagreement between analysts and errors in complex cases. The 2009 National Academy report criticised the field's foundations, and research continues on the physics.

Where it stands

Documented: the fluid dynamics.

Contested: the reconstruction.

Documented: analyst disagreement.

The honest label: live debate.

Why it is in the archive

Because the physics is sound and the story told from it is a separate step.
