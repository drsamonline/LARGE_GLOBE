Predatory Journals

Journals that charge authors and publish without real review. The category is documented and its boundaries are argued.

The practice

Predatory journals solicit submissions, charge article-processing fees and publish quickly with little or no peer review. Investigations have shown that fabricated papers are accepted, and that editors may be listed without consent.

Maps, diagrams and images

The boundary problem

The label is contested because it is applied unevenly, sometimes to legitimate journals in developing countries with limited resources. Researchers have documented the harm and criticised the term for that reason.
