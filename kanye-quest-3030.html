Kanye Quest 3030

A fan-made role-playing game with a hidden room that led players to a real website and a recruitment pitch for a 'clone' cult.

The game

Kanye Quest 3030 is a free fan-made role-playing game released in 2013, in which the player controls a version of Kanye West through a surreal future. It attracted attention in 2015 when players found a hidden area with references to a group calling itself 'Ascension' and a link to an external website.

Maps, diagrams and images

The aftermath

The hidden content was investigated and the site was found to promote a fringe belief about cloning. The game's creator removed the content and the episode is documented in reporting on the game and on the group.

The archive files it as documented because the game, the hidden content and the reporting all exist.

What is established

Documented: the game and the hidden area.

Documented: the linked website and its claims.

Documented: the creator's removal of the content.

Not established: any wider significance.

The label: documented.

Why it is in the archive

Because it is a real case of a fan project carrying hidden content that led somewhere unexpected.
