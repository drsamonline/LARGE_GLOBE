The Anunnaki

Sitchin's claim that alien visitors created humanity, built on a mistranslation of Sumerian texts.

The claim

Zecharia Sitchin argued that Sumerian texts describe astronauts from a planet called Nibiru who genetically engineered humans to work in mines. The claim was built on his own translations of cuneiform.

Maps, diagrams and images

The evidence

Assyriologists have rejected the translations: the Sumerian term he rendered as 'rocket' means a type of boat, and the 'planets' he counted do not match the texts. The Anunnaki are deities in the Mesopotamian pantheon, and the texts are documented and translated by specialists.

What is established

Documented: the Sumerian texts.

Documented: the scholarly translations.

Rejected: Sitchin's readings.

Not established: any alien contact.

The label: debunked.

Why it is in the archive

Because it is the most influential pseudo-translation of the century, and the error is checkable.
