SCP Is a Real Agency

The SCP Foundation is a collaborative fiction wiki. The claim that it is a real agency with real containment procedures is a misunderstanding of a very consistent joke.

The claim

The SCP Foundation is a collaborative writing project, founded in 2007, in which contributors write clinical-style documents about anomalous objects and containment procedures. The claim that it is a real organisation appears periodically, usually because the documents are written in the register of official paperwork.

Maps, diagrams and images

The facts

The wiki states its fictional nature, publishes under a Creative Commons licence, and has no institutional existence. Its 'containment procedures' are narrative devices, and its 'classified' material is publicly readable.

The archive files it as fiction and keeps it beside the pages where a fictional document has been mistaken for a real one.

What is established

Documented: the wiki, its licence and its format.

Documented: its fictional nature.

Not established: any real agency.

The mechanism: a documentary register.

The label: fiction.

Why it is in the archive

Because it is the largest body of fiction in the format that real claims use, and because the label is on the front page.
