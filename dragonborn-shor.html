The Dragonborn Is Shor

A theory about the player character's identity in a game's mythology. It is an interpretation of a fiction.

The theory

The theory holds that the player character is an incarnation of a god in the game's pantheon, based on the game's prophecies and the role of the dragon-blooded.

Maps, diagrams and images

The status

The game does not confirm it, and the reading competes with others. Filed as fiction.

What is established

Documented: the game and its lore.

Documented: the reading.

Not canon: unconfirmed.

The label: fiction.

Why it is in the archive

Because it is a reading that takes the fiction's own mythology seriously.
