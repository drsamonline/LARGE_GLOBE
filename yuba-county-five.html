The Yuba County Five

Five men drove into the mountains after a basketball game and were found dead months later. What happened in between has never been established.

The case

In February 1978, five men with mild intellectual disabilities left a basketball game in Chico, California, and drove into the Sierra Nevada. Their car was found days later, abandoned on a forest road, with the keys in the ignition and no sign of the men. The first body was found months later, and others followed.

The explanations

Theories have included exposure, an accident, and a third party. A key detail is that the car was drivable and the road led back to safety, which makes the decision to leave it unexplained. A 2020 documentary revived attention, and no explanation has been established.

The case is documented in police records and remains unresolved.

Where it stands

Documented: the disappearance and the recovery of the bodies.

Documented: the state of the car and the road.

Not established: the sequence of events.

Not established: any third party.

The honest label: live debate.

Why it is in the archive

Because it is a real disappearance where the physical evidence is intact and the story is not.
