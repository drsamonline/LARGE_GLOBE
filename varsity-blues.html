Varsity Blues

A college admissions scandal in which wealthy parents paid for fraudulent athletic recruitment. The case is documented in indictments and convictions.

The case

In 2019 the US Department of Justice indicted dozens of people in a scheme to secure university places through fraudulent athletic recruitment, bribes to coaches and falsified test scores. The operation was called Varsity Blues.

Maps, diagrams and images

The outcome

The case produced convictions, including of the organiser, William Singer, and of parents and coaches. The sentences and the colleges' responses are documented, and the case prompted changes in admissions oversight.

The archive files it as documented because the indictments and convictions are public.
