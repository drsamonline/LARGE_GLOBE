Doofenshmirtz Is Phineas's Father

A theory that the cartoon villain is the boys' father. It is an interpretation, and the show has not confirmed it.

The theory

The theory notes physical resemblances and narrative parallels between the villain and the boys' unseen father, and proposes that the show has hidden the relationship in plain sight.

Maps, diagrams and images

The status

The show's creators have not confirmed it, and the series is episodic comedy in which continuity is loose. The reading is a fan construction, filed as fiction.

What is established

Documented: the show and the parallels.

Documented: the theory.

Not canon: unconfirmed.

The label: fiction.

Why it is in the archive

Because it is a harmless reading and the archive labels harmless readings as fiction rather than error.
