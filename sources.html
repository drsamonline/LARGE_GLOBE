How to use this bibliography

If you want to check a specific sentence, find the dossier it came from and follow the matching link. If a claim in the archive is not backed by one of these sources, it does not belong here — tell me and it will be removed or fixed.

For the creationist and flat-Earth material, the fringe sources are listed on purpose. The right way to assess an argument is to read it as its author wrote it, then read the response, then decide.
