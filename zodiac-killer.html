The Zodiac Killer

Five confirmed murders in California, a set of ciphers sent to newspapers, and a case that has never been closed. Two of the ciphers have been solved.

The case

Between 1968 and 1969, a series of murders in northern California was attributed to a killer who sent letters and ciphers to newspapers, calling himself the Zodiac. Five killings are confirmed; the killer claimed more. The letters stopped in the 1970s, and the case remains open.

The ciphers

The 408-character cipher was solved in 1969 by a schoolteacher couple. The 340-character cipher was solved in 2020 by an international team of amateur codebreakers, and it contained no name. A third, the 13-character cipher, is unsolved.

A leading suspect, Arthur Leigh Allen, was never charged and died in 1992; later analyses have pointed both towards and away from him. The case is documented but unresolved.

Where it stands

Documented: the murders, the letters and the ciphers.

Solved: two ciphers, neither naming the killer.

Not established: the identity.

Not charged: any suspect.

The honest label: live debate.

Why it is in the archive

Because it is a real unsolved crime where the puzzles have been solved and the question has not.
