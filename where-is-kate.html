Where Is Kate

Weeks of speculation about a public figure's absence, ended by an announcement and a diagnosis. A case study in how rumours fill a vacuum.

The sequence

In early 2024 the Princess of Wales withdrew from public duties after abdominal surgery, and the palace gave limited information. Weeks of speculation followed, amplified by a manipulated family photograph that news agencies withdrew.

Maps, diagrams and images

The outcome

She announced in March 2024 that she was undergoing treatment for cancer, and returned to public duties later in the year. The episode is documented in the reporting and in the agencies' withdrawal notices.

The archive files it as documented, and treats it as a case study in what fills an information vacuum.

What is established

Documented: the absence and the statements.

Documented: the withdrawn photograph.

Documented: the diagnosis announcement.

Not established: the conspiracy theories.

The label: documented.

Why it is in the archive

Because it is a recent case where the vacuum was filled in public and the filling is the lesson.
