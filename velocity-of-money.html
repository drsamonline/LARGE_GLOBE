The Velocity of Money

How fast money circulates, and why its apparent collapse puzzled economists.

The concept

Velocity is the ratio of nominal output to the money stock — how many times a unit of money is spent in a period. It is documented in monetary statistics and features in the equation of exchange.

Maps, diagrams and images

The puzzle

Velocity fell sharply after 2008 in several economies, which simple monetarist predictions did not anticipate. Explanations include excess reserves, changed payment behaviour and measurement problems with broad money, and the interpretation is documented as unsettled.
