Swimming After Eating

The rule that you must wait an hour after eating before swimming. There is no evidence for it.

The advice

The advice to wait an hour after eating before swimming is repeated at pools and beaches. It rests on the idea that blood diverted to digestion causes cramps and drowning.

Maps, diagrams and images

The evidence

No study has shown an increased risk of drowning after eating, and the physiological effect is minor. Authorities in several countries have dropped the advice, though they still recommend supervision and caution.

What is established

Documented: the advice.

Not found: increased risk.

Dropped: by several authorities.

The label: debunked.

Why it is in the archive

Because it is advice that has been followed for a century without a study behind it.
