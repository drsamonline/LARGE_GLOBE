The 1MDB Heist

Billions taken from a Malaysian state fund, traced through banks, films and a yacht. The prosecutions are documented.

The scheme

1MDB was a Malaysian state investment fund. Investigators in several countries traced billions of dollars diverted from it into private accounts, luxury goods, a yacht, a film production company and political donations, through layered transactions across jurisdictions.

Maps, diagrams and images

The outcome

The US Department of Justice described the largest kleptocracy asset recovery action it had brought. Prosecutions and settlements followed, including the financier Jho Low, who remains at large, and a former prime minister was convicted in Malaysia.

Why it is in the archive

Because it is a conspiracy that really existed, and the evidence is a paper trail across five countries.
