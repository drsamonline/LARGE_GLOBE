Kevin Is Jigsaw

The theory that the boy in Home Alone grew up to be the killer in Saw. It is a joke that got elaborate, and it is not in either film.

The theory

The theory holds that Kevin McCallister, the child who defends his house with improvised traps in Home Alone, grows up to become John Kramer's successor — or Kramer himself — in Saw. The supporting argument is the ingenuity of the traps, the isolation, and the willingness to injure adults who invade his home.

Maps, diagrams and images

The response

The theory is a fan joke that became elaborate. There is no connection between the films: different studios, different creators, different genres, and a timeline that does not work. The creators of neither franchise have endorsed it.

The archive files it as fiction because that is what it is — an inventive reading, offered as entertainment.

What is established

Documented: the theory and its arguments.

Not established: any connection between the films.

Documented: the separate production histories.

The value: a demonstration of how far a reading can be pushed.

The label: fiction.

Why it is in the archive

Because it is the purest example of a theory built entirely from pattern-matching between unrelated texts.
