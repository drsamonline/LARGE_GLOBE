The Spear of Destiny

A spear said to have pierced Christ, and a legend that its holder commands the world. The legend is modern.

The objects

Several spearheads are identified as the Holy Lance, including one in Vienna's Imperial Treasury. The identification rests on tradition rather than on evidence, and the objects cannot all be the same.

Maps, diagrams and images

The legend

The idea that the spear grants victory to its holder is a twentieth-century elaboration, popularised in occult writing and attached to Hitler by later authors. No source before the modern period describes such a power.

The archive files it as fiction: the objects are real antiquities, and the legend is a modern story.

What is established

Documented: the objects and their traditions.

Not established: which, if any, is authentic.

Modern: the legend of power.

No source: before the 20th century.

The label: fiction.

Why it is in the archive

Because it is the classic relic legend, and the legend has a date.
