Saki Sanobashi

A lost anime said to show children trapped in a bathroom. It has never been found, and the search suggests it never existed.

The claim

Saki Sanobashi — also called 'Go for a Punch' — is said to be a 1980s or 1990s anime in which a group of girls are trapped in a bathroom and descend into violence. The claim appeared on 4chan around 2015, and a search community formed to find it.

Maps, diagrams and images

The search

The search has produced no tape, no studio catalogue entry, no broadcast record and no still. Several people have come forward claiming to have seen it, with inconsistent descriptions. The consensus among lost-media researchers is that it is a fabrication, or a memory of a different work.

The archive files it as fiction because the object does not exist, and because the search is the documented part.

What is established

Documented: the claim and the search.

Documented: the absence of any record.

Not found: any tape or still.

Likely: a fabrication or a memory blend.

The label: fiction.

Why it is in the archive

Because it is the cleanest lost-media case where the absence of evidence is the conclusion.
