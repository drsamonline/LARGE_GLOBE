The Saraswati River

A river praised in the Vedas and identified by some with a dry channel in Haryana. The palaeochannel is real; the identification is argued.

The evidence

Satellite and sediment studies document palaeochannels in Haryana and Rajasthan, including the Ghaggar-Hakra system, which carried water in the Holocene and dried as the monsoon weakened. The Vedas praise a river called Saraswati.

Maps, diagrams and images

The argument

Some scholars identify the Ghaggar-Hakra with the Vedic Saraswati and place Vedic culture on its banks; others argue the identification is uncertain, that the river's description is partly mythical, and that the chronology does not fit. Both are documented.

Where it stands

Documented: the palaeochannels.

Documented: the drying.

Contested: the identification.

The honest label: live debate.

Why it is in the archive

Because it is a real geological feature carrying a very heavy textual claim.
