Tryptophan and Turkey

The claim that turkey makes you sleepy. Turkey has no more tryptophan than chicken, and the meal is the cause.

The claim

The claim holds that the tryptophan in turkey causes post-dinner drowsiness at Thanksgiving. Tryptophan is a precursor of serotonin and melatonin, which are involved in sleep.

Maps, diagrams and images

The evidence

Turkey contains tryptophan in amounts comparable to chicken and other meats, and the large carbohydrate-rich meal, alcohol and the time of day are better explanations. Tryptophan crosses into the brain more readily with carbohydrates, which complicates the simple story.

What is established

Documented: tryptophan in turkey.

Documented: comparable levels in other meats.

Better explanation: meal size and alcohol.

The label: debunked.

Why it is in the archive

Because it is a nutrition claim that a food-composition table settles.
