The Bhavishya Purana

A purana cited for prophecies about later figures. Its predictive sections have a documented history of insertion.

The claims

Sections of the Bhavishya Purana are cited as predicting the arrival of figures from later history, including Islamic and European ones, and sometimes modern events.

Maps, diagrams and images

The evidence

Scholars have documented that the text is composite, with sections added over centuries, and that the 'prophecies' match events that had already happened at the time of composition. The text is real; its predictive sections are late insertions.

Where it stands

Documented: the text and its composite nature.

Documented: the late additions.

Not predictive: the matching sections.

The honest label: fringe.

Why it is in the archive

Because it is a real scripture used for a claim the text cannot support.
