A White Dwarf as a Weapon

The claim that a stellar remnant could be steered, or used, as a weapon. It appears in science fiction and in UFO lore; the physics of moving a star is the objection.

The claim

The idea appears in two forms: science fiction, where stellar remnants are engineered into weapons, and UFO literature, where a white dwarf — often Sirius B — is said to have been used or threatened. The claim is that a sufficiently advanced civilisation could accelerate a white dwarf and direct it at a target.

Maps, diagrams and images

The physics

A white dwarf has roughly the mass of the Sun compressed into an Earth-sized body. Changing its velocity requires momentum, and momentum requires either ejecting mass at high speed or an external force; both require energy on a scale that no known process supplies at the required rate. The object is also a star, with its own orbital dynamics and a galaxy of objects to interact with.

There is no observation of an anomalous white dwarf trajectory, and no mechanism proposed beyond assertion.

Where it stands

Documented: white dwarfs, their masses and their dynamics.

Not established: any means of steering one.

Not observed: any anomalous stellar trajectory.

Not testable: as stated.

The honest label: fringe — fiction and lore with a physics objection attached.

Why it is in the archive

Because it is the largest object anyone has proposed as a weapon, and because the objection is a momentum budget rather than a mystery.
