The Silence of the Indus Script

A script on thousands of seals from a major civilisation, and no bilingual text to unlock it.

The corpus

The Indus Valley Civilisation produced thousands of inscribed seals and objects with a script of several hundred signs. The inscriptions are short, often five signs, and no long text or bilingual has been found.

Maps, diagrams and images

The problem

Without a bilingual, decipherment depends on assumptions about the language, and proposals have claimed Dravidian, Indo-Aryan, Munda and non-linguistic readings. None is accepted, and the shortness of the texts is the central obstacle.

What is established

Documented: the corpus and its signs.

Documented: the absence of a bilingual.

Not accepted: any decipherment.

The obstacle: short texts.

The label: documented.

Why it is in the archive

Because it is the largest undeciphered script of a major civilisation, and the reason is structural.
