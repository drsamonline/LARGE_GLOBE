Soma

A ritual drink praised in the Rigveda, and a botanical identification that has never been settled.

The hymns

The Rigveda contains many hymns to soma, a plant and a drink used in ritual, described as pressed, mixed with milk and offered to the gods. The identity of the plant has been argued for two centuries.

Maps, diagrams and images

The candidates

Proposals include a mushroom, ephedra, a species of milkweed, and a plant that has since become unavailable. Each has textual or geographical support and problems. The Iranian tradition uses a related word for a different preparation, which complicates the identification.

The archive files it as live debate because the texts are documented and the plant is not identified.

Where it stands

Documented: the hymns and the ritual.

Documented: the preparation described.

Contested: the plant.

Many candidates: none conclusive.

The honest label: live debate.

Why it is in the archive

Because it is a lost plant at the centre of the oldest hymns in the tradition.
