The Fleshgait and the Wendigo

Two creatures from different traditions, run together on the internet. One is an internet invention; the other belongs to Algonquian peoples.

The two

The wendigo belongs to the traditions of Algonquian-speaking peoples, associated with winter, hunger and cannibalism; it is a serious figure in those traditions, not a monster for stories. The fleshgait is an internet invention of the 2010s, described as a creature that mimics voices and replaces people.

Maps, diagrams and images

The problem

Internet horror often treats the two as the same kind of thing, which flattens a living tradition into content. The fleshgait has no tradition behind it at all.

The archive files both as fiction, and separates them, because the difference matters to the people whose tradition one of them is.

What is established

Documented: the Algonquian tradition.

Documented: the internet invention.

Not established: either creature as an animal.

The issue: a tradition used as content.

The label: fiction.

Why it is in the archive

Because it is where the archive's method meets an ethical question rather than a factual one.
