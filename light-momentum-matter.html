Light's Momentum in Matter

Does a photon's momentum in a medium follow Minkowski's or Abraham's formula? The argument ran for a century.

The debate

Two expressions for the momentum of light in a dielectric — Abraham's and Minkowski's — differ by a factor of the refractive index. Both have been defended, and the disagreement ran for a century because the two describe different things.

Maps, diagrams and images

The resolution

Experiments and theory now distinguish the momentum of the field from the momentum imparted to the medium, and the apparent contradiction dissolves once the medium's motion is included. The history is documented as a case study in how a semantic dispute can look like a physical one.

Where it stands

Documented: both formulas.

Documented: the experiments.

Largely resolved: by distinguishing the quantities.

Instructive: as a case study.

The honest label: live debate.

Why it is in the archive

Because it is a hundred-year argument that turned out to be about what the question meant.
