Deadpool's Fourth Wall

The character talks to the reader. The convention is documented; the 'proof' it gives about the character's world is not.

The convention

Deadpool is written as aware that he is a comic character, addressing the reader and the creators. The convention is documented and is central to the character's comedy.

Maps, diagrams and images

The problem

The convention is a narrative device, not evidence about the character's world. Arguments that it proves the character is in a simulation, or that he is the only 'real' being, treat a device as a fact — the same move as the theories about sitcom purgatories.

The archive files it as fiction and notes that the character's awareness is the joke.

What is established

Documented: the convention.

Documented: its use in the comics and films.

Not a claim: about anything real.

The device: metafiction.

The label: fiction.

Why it is in the archive

Because metafiction is the one convention that readers most often mistake for evidence.
