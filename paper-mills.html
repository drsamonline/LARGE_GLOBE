Paper Mills

Organisations that sell authorship on fabricated papers. Their output is documented in the published record.

The industry

Paper mills produce fabricated or manipulated manuscripts and sell authorship to researchers who need publications. Investigators have identified thousands of papers with shared templates, duplicated images and improbable data, and retractions have followed.

Maps, diagrams and images

The response

Publishers and integrity researchers have documented the patterns and built detection tools; retraction notices and investigations are public. The scale is estimated rather than measured, because only detected cases are counted.
