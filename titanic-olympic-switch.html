The Titanic–Olympic Switch

A claim that the Titanic was swapped with its sister ship for the insurance. The ships' records settle it.

The claim

The claim holds that the White Star Line switched the Titanic with its damaged sister Olympic, sank the Olympic deliberately and claimed the insurance on the Titanic. It appears in books and documentaries.

Maps, diagrams and images

The problems

The ships had different hull numbers, fittings and dimensions, and thousands of photographs, plans, crew records and passenger accounts document both. The wreck's identification, the debris field and the physical evidence match the Titanic. The insurance claim was modest compared with the loss.

The archive files it as debunked.

What is established

Documented: both ships' records and photographs.

Documented: the wreck's identification.

Not established: any switch.

Implausible: the logistics and the motive.

The label: debunked.

Why it is in the archive

Because it is a conspiracy theory with a paper trail that refutes it completely.
