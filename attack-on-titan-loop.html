Attack on Titan's Time Loop

A theory that the story repeats. It is an interpretation of a manga and anime.

The theory

The theory reads the series' later revelations about memory and future knowledge as a time loop, with events repeating until a condition is met.

Maps, diagrams and images

The status

The series has a definitive ending that addresses determinism without a literal loop; the theory is one reading of many. Filed as fiction.

What is established

Documented: the work and its ending.

Documented: the reading.

Not canon: a literal loop.

The label: fiction.

Why it is in the archive

Because it is a case where the ending answered the question and the theory survived anyway.
