Breaking Bad and The Walking Dead

A theory that the two shows share a universe, built on a bag of blue meth and a timeline. It is a joke that got elaborate.

The theory

The theory holds that Breaking Bad and The Walking Dead take place in the same world, with Walter White's meth as the cause of the outbreak — or at least with the shows sharing a timeline and a drug trade. Supporters cite a bag of blue meth that appears in The Walking Dead.

Maps, diagrams and images

The problems

The blue meth was an Easter egg placed by the shows' shared network, and the creators of both have treated it as a nod rather than canon. The timelines and settings do not align, and no episode establishes a connection.

The archive files it as fiction and notes the mechanism: an in-joke read as continuity.

What is established

Documented: the Easter egg.

Documented: the theory.

Not established: any shared universe.

The mechanism: a nod read as canon.

The label: fiction.

Why it is in the archive

Because it is the clearest case of a prop becoming a cosmology.
