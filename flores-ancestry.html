The Flores Hobbit's Ancestry

Homo floresiensis is accepted as a species; where it came from is still argued.

The species

Homo floresiensis, the metre-tall hominin from Liang Bua on Flores, is accepted as a distinct species, dated to between roughly 100,000 and 50,000 years ago, with primitive wrist anatomy and small brain.

Maps, diagrams and images

The ancestry

Proposals include descent from early Homo erectus, from an earlier hominin such as Homo habilis, or from a lineage that left Africa before erectus. Recent work on the Mata Menge fossils suggests a small-bodied ancestor, which supports deep ancestry; the question is not closed.
