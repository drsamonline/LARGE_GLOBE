The Pentagon's UAP Programme

A defence department programme to investigate unidentified objects, disclosed in 2017 and later renamed and expanded.

The programme

The Advanced Aerospace Threat Identification Program ran from 2007 within the US Department of Defense, funded at the request of a senator, and investigated reports of unidentified aerial phenomena. Its existence was reported in 2017.

Maps, diagrams and images

The aftermath

The programme was succeeded by a task force and then an office, and reports have been published. The office has found no evidence of extraterrestrial technology, while some cases remain unidentified. The documents and statements are on the record.

What is established

Documented: the programme and its funding.

Documented: the successor offices.

Documented: the published reports.

Not established: any extraterrestrial technology.

The label: documented.

Why it is in the archive

Because it is the case where the government's interest is documented and the mystery is not solved.
