The Astros Sign Stealing

A baseball team used a camera and a monitor to relay the opposing catcher's signals. The scheme is documented in a league report.

The scheme

During the 2017 season the Houston Astros used a camera in centre field to read the opposing catcher's signs and relay them to the batter by banging on a trash can. The scheme was described in reporting and confirmed by a league investigation in 2020.

Maps, diagrams and images

The penalties

The league fined the club, stripped draft picks and suspended the manager and general manager, who were then dismissed by the team. Players received immunity in exchange for testimony and were not punished.
