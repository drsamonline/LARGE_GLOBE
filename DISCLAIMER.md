# Disclaimer

The Larger World Archive · version 1.0 · effective 23 November 2026

The archive exists to show what is claimed, what is documented, and what the evidence
supports. It is a reference work about claims, not an authority on any subject it
covers, and nothing on it is advice.

## 1. Not professional advice

Nothing here is medical, legal, financial, psychological, safety or engineering advice.
A dossier on a disease, a treatment, a supplement, a legal theory or a financial scheme
describes what has been claimed and what the record shows. It is not a recommendation
to act, and it is not a substitute for a qualified professional who knows your
circumstances. If a page concerns a health condition, speak to a clinician; if it
concerns a legal or financial decision, speak to a lawyer or a regulated adviser.

## 2. Labels are about evidence, not worth

A `Debunked` label means a specific claim failed a specific test. It does not mean the
people who made it were dishonest, or that every related question is closed. A
`Fringe claim` label means the evidence is absent rather than contradicted — the claim
is unproven, not disproven. A `Live debate` label means serious people disagree and the
disagreement is documented; it does not mean both sides are equally supported.

## 3. Sources are linked, not guaranteed

Every dossier lists its sources, and the links are checked periodically. A dead link
means the source has moved or been withdrawn, not that the claim has changed. Where a
page relies on a secondary summary, it says so. Where the record is thin, the label
says so.

## 4. Images are illustrative

Photographs are chosen to show the subject of a page — an object, a place, a document,
a person — and come from Wikimedia Commons under free licences. They are not evidence
for a claim. A photograph of a stone circle does not establish who built it, and a
drawing made centuries after an event is a drawing, not a record.

Diagrams are drawn by the archive to show the structure of an argument. They are not
reconstructions of anything physical, and they are not maps.

## 5. Fiction is labelled as fiction

Pages about films, games, books and internet stories are labelled `Fiction` where nobody
is claiming the events happened. A page analysing a fan theory is analysing an
interpretation, not asserting it.

The site is provided as-is, without warranty of any kind. It is published in good faith
and checked against its own automated tests, but errors are possible. If you rely on
something here for a decision that matters, verify it against the primary source linked

arising from use of the site.

## 7. Corrections

Errors are corrected on the page and logged on the
[corrections page](https://drsamonline.github.io/LARGE_GLOBE/corrections.html), with
what was wrong and what replaced it. Reporting one is welcome; it is the point of the
exercise rather than an embarrassment.
