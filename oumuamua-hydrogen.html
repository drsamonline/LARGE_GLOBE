‘Oumuamua as Hydrogen Ice

An explanation for the object's odd acceleration: it was a chunk of hydrogen ice. Elegant, and hard to test now that it has gone.

The proposal

In 2023 two researchers proposed that 'Oumuamua was a body of molecular hydrogen ice, which would sublimate slowly and produce the small non-gravitational acceleration observed without the dust coma that water ice would create.

The problems

The model explains the acceleration and the absence of a coma, and it requires that hydrogen ice bodies form in molecular clouds — which is plausible and unobserved. The object cannot be re-observed, so the proposal cannot be confirmed, only judged on its consistency.

The archive files it as live debate.

Where it stands

Documented: the observation.

Proposed: hydrogen ice, 2023.

Consistent: with the data.

Untestable: without the object.

The honest label: live debate.

Why it is in the archive

Because it is a good explanation for something we can no longer look at.
