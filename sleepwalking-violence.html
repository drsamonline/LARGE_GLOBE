Sleepwalking Violence

People have committed violence while asleep, and courts have had to decide what that means.

The phenomenon

Sleepwalking and other non-REM parasomnias can involve complex behaviour, and a small number of criminal cases have involved violence during an episode. Several have ended in acquittal on the basis that the act was not voluntary.

Maps, diagrams and images

The evidence

The cases are documented in sleep-medicine and legal literature, and the diagnosis is made with sleep studies and history. The phenomenon is real and rare, and courts have sometimes accepted it and sometimes not.
