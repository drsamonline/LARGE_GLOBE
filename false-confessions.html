False Confessions

People confess to crimes they did not commit, and the reasons are documented.

The phenomenon

DNA exonerations have documented cases of people who confessed to serious crimes they did not commit, sometimes with details, and sometimes after hours of interrogation.

Maps, diagrams and images

The causes

Documented mechanisms include compliance under pressure, internalisation of guilt during long interrogations, minimisation of consequences, and the use of false evidence ploys. Recording of interrogations and limits on techniques are documented reforms.

What is established

Documented: the cases.

The label: documented.

Why it is in the archive

Because a confession feels like the strongest evidence and is sometimes the weakest.
