The Flannan Isles Lighthouse

Three lighthouse keepers vanished from a Hebridean island in 1900. The lamps were trimmed, the kitchen was abandoned mid-meal, and no bodies were found.

The event

In December 1900 the lighthouse at Eilean Mòr in the Flannan Isles was found abandoned. All three keepers had vanished. Inside, the lamps were cleaned and refilled, and there were signs that a meal had been interrupted. Damage to the landing stage suggested a storm.

Maps, diagrams and images

The explanation

The Northern Lighthouse Board's investigation concluded that the men had gone to secure equipment at the west landing during heavy seas and been swept away by an exceptional wave. Later research has supported this, with debate about whether one man went first and the others followed.

The story has attracted supernatural explanations since; the documented account is a storm and a dangerous coast.

What is established

Documented: the abandonment and the state of the station.

Documented: storm damage at the landing stage.

Concluded: by the Board, as a wave.

Not found: the bodies.

The label: documented.

Why it is in the archive

Because it is the classic disappearance with a mundane official finding that the stories ignore.
