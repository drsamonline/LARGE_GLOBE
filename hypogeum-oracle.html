The Hypogeum Oracle Room

A subterranean chamber in Malta that resonates at a specific frequency. The acoustics are measured; the ritual use is inferred.

The site

The Hal Saflieni Hypogeum in Malta is a prehistoric underground complex cut into rock, with chambers including one known as the Oracle Room, which has painted red ochre spirals and unusual acoustic properties.

Maps, diagrams and images

The research

Measurements have found strong resonance in the low frequency range in some chambers, and researchers have proposed that the spaces were used for ritual involving sound or chanting. The acoustics are documented; the ritual use is inference from the architecture and the art.

Where it stands

Documented: the chambers and the art.

Measured: the resonance.

Inferred: the ritual use.

Not established: any specific practice.

The honest label: live debate.

Why it is in the archive

Because it is a case where the physics is measured and the meaning is guessed.
