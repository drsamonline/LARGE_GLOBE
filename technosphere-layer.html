The Technosphere Layer

The claim that human technology has become a geological layer of the planet, with its own mass and dynamics. The concept is now used in Earth-systems science.

The concept

The technosphere is the global system of human-made materials, infrastructure and processes, considered as a layer of the Earth system comparable to the biosphere. The term was developed by Peter Haff and others, and it is now used in discussions of the Anthropocene.

Maps, diagrams and images

The measurement

Estimates of the technosphere's mass — buildings, roads, machines, waste — put it in the order of trillions of tonnes, and studies have found that human-made mass now exceeds the total biomass. The figures are estimates with wide uncertainty, and the concept is a framework rather than a measurement.

The archive files it as documented because the concept and the estimates are in the literature.

Why it is in the archive

Because it is a case where a new concept has changed how a whole field describes the planet, and the numbers are still being argued.
