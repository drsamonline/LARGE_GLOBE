Cars Wiped Out Humanity

A Pixar shared-universe theory in which the cars inherited a world the humans left. It is a joke, and the films do not say it.

The theory

The theory holds that Cars takes place after humanity left or died out, with the vehicles as the inheritors of the planet, and that this places it in a shared continuity with other Pixar films. It circulated widely as a 'Pixar theory'.

Maps, diagrams and images

The problems

The films present a world of living machines with its own history and no reference to human extinction. The theory is an exercise in connecting unrelated films, and Pixar has not endorsed it; the studio's films do contain in-jokes, which is a different thing.

The archive files it as fiction and notes the method: shared-universe building from Easter eggs.

What is established

Documented: the theory and the in-jokes cited.

Documented: the films' own settings.

Not established: a shared continuity.

The method: Easter eggs as canon.

The label: fiction.

Why it is in the archive

Because it is the most elaborate example of a fan cosmology built from background details.
