The East Palestine Burn

A derailment, then a controlled burn of vinyl chloride that sent a plume over the town. Both are documented.

The event

In February 2023 a freight train derailed in East Palestine, Ohio, carrying hazardous materials including vinyl chloride. Officials conducted a controlled vent and burn of the chemical to prevent an explosion, producing a large plume of smoke over the town.

Maps, diagrams and images

The aftermath

Residents reported symptoms; environmental monitoring and independent testing documented contamination of soil and water; federal and state agencies and a class action followed. Studies of long-term health effects are ongoing.

The archive files it as documented.

Why it is in the archive
