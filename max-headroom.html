The Max Headroom Incident

Two Chicago television stations were hijacked by a masked figure in 1987. Nobody was ever charged.

The incident

In November 1987, a signal intruder interrupted two Chicago television broadcasts. The first, during a news programme, transmitted a few seconds of buzzing and a figure; the second, during a science-fiction drama, ran for over a minute with a man in a Max Headroom mask and a corset, speaking nonsense and being spanked with a flyswatter.

Maps, diagrams and images

The investigation

The broadcasts were traced to a microwave signal intrusion, which requires equipment and line-of-sight to the receiver. The FBI investigated and no one was charged; the case remains open, and the culprits have never been identified.

The incident is documented on tape — the recordings survive — and it remains the best-known broadcast hijack.

What is established

Documented: the intrusion and the recordings.

Documented: the method, microwave injection.

Not established: the identity.

Investigated: by the FBI.

The label: documented.

Why it is in the archive

Because it is a real hijack with a real recording and no culprit, which is the rare combination.
