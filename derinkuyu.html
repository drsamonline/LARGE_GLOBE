Derinkuyu

An underground city in Cappadocia with rooms, stables, a church and ventilation shafts, deep enough for thousands of people.

The city

Derinkuyu in Cappadocia, Turkey, is an underground city cut into soft volcanic tuff, extending to a depth of about 85 metres with levels for storage, stables, a church, schools and living quarters. It is one of several such sites in the region.

Maps, diagrams and images

The engineering

The site's achievement is ventilation: vertical shafts carried air through the levels, and the tunnels and doors could be closed with large stone wheels. The city was used over centuries, with the deepest levels probably cut in the Byzantine period as refuge during raids.

The site is documented in surveys and is open to visitors.
