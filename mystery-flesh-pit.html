The Mystery Flesh Pit

A worldbuilding project about a national park inside a living organism. It is fiction, presented as an archive of a place that never existed.

The project

Mystery Flesh Pit National Park is a worldbuilding project, begun in 2019, presented as an archive of a Texan national park built inside the body of a vast ancient organism. It includes maps, safety notices, corporate documents and a disaster report.

Maps, diagrams and images

The status

The project is fiction, made by a creator who has discussed it, and its effect comes from the bureaucratic format: the park service notices, the liability waivers, the incident reports. It is the same technique as the SCP wiki and analog horror, applied to landscape.

The archive files it as fiction and notes the format, which is the interesting part.

What is established

Documented: the project and its creator.

Documented: the documentary format.

Not a claim: about the world.

The technique: bureaucratic realism.

The label: fiction.

Why it is in the archive

Because it is the best example of worldbuilding that borrows the authority of official paperwork.
