Support the archive

Nothing here is for sale.
That is the point.

The Larger World Archive is one person reading, checking and writing, with no ads, no accounts, no analytics and no sponsor with a veto over a finding. Every dossier, every source link and every image credit stays free. If it saved you an afternoon, you can pay for one.

What the money actually covers: the domain and hosting, the licences and fees for the credited images, the books and papers that are not open access, and the hours it takes to trace a claim back to the person who first made it. What it does not buy: influence over a label. A claim that fails its test is marked Debunked whether or not anybody sponsored the page.

FreeWhat helps more than money

Three things cost nothing and are worth more than a donation:

  • Report a dead source. Two thousand external links rot quietly. If one is gone, send the page and the link and it gets replaced or annotated.
  • Send the primary text. A first edition, a scanned paper, a court filing — if a claim is quoted second-hand anywhere in the archive, the original is better.
  • Star the repository. drsamonline/LARGE_GLOBE — it is how other people find the archive at all.

Support is voluntary and buys nothing except more dossiers. No receipt is issued, no tier exists, and no part of the archive is ever placed behind it.

Honest accountingWhere it goes

Hosting is GitHub Pages, which costs nothing. The recurring costs are a domain, the image licences for the 167 credited photographs, and access to paywalled literature. The rest is time. When a month's support exceeds those costs, the surplus buys scans and pays for archive access — not hardware, not advertising, and never a sponsored label.
