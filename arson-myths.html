Arson Myths

Investigators read patterns in fire damage as proof of arson. Many of the signs were normal fire behaviour.

The myths

For decades, investigators testified that crazed glass, deep charring, 'alligatoring' and multiple origins indicated accelerants and arson. Those indicators were based on experience rather than experiment.

Maps, diagrams and images

The correction

Full-scale fire experiments in the 1980s and 1990s showed that these patterns occur in ordinary fires. Reviews documented wrongful arson convictions, and several have been overturned. The episode is documented in fire-science literature.

What is established

Documented: the indicators used.

Documented: the experiments.

Not valid: the indicators.

Documented: the wrongful convictions.

The label: debunked.

Why it is in the archive

Because it is a case where experience was mistaken for evidence, and people were convicted on it.
