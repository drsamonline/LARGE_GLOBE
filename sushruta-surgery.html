Sushruta's Surgery

A Sanskrit surgical text describing operations including rhinoplasty. The text is real and its procedures were performed.

The text

The Sushruta Samhita describes surgical instruments, procedures including cataract surgery and reconstructive rhinoplasty, and pre-operative preparation. Its date is argued, with a core from the middle of the first millennium BC and later additions.

Maps, diagrams and images

The evidence

The text is documented in manuscript tradition, and reconstructive nose surgery using cheek or forehead flaps was practised in India and described by later European observers. The claim that Indian surgeons performed these operations is supported; claims of modern standards are not.

Why it is in the archive

Because it is the strongest case in the archive for ancient technical achievement, and it needs no exaggeration.
