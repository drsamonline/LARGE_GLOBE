FTX

Billions in customer deposits were lent to a trading firm run by the founder's girlfriend, moved through code exemptions, and spent on venture bets and real estate.

The structure

FTX was an exchange: customers deposited crypto and dollars to trade. Alameda Research was a trading firm, also founded by Sam Bankman-Fried and run by Caroline Ellison. The two were supposed to be separate.

Maps, diagrams and images

In November 2022, a leaked balance sheet showed Alameda's assets were largely illiquid tokens issued by FTX itself. A rival exchange's decision to sell its holdings triggered a run, a rescue deal collapsed, and FTX filed for bankruptcy within days.

What the investigation found

The bankruptcy estate's investigators found that customer deposits had been transferred to Alameda to cover trading losses, venture investments and political donations. A code feature in FTX's systems allowed Alameda to carry a negative balance — effectively an unlimited line of credit from customer funds.

Bankman-Fried was convicted in November 2023 on seven counts including wire fraud and money laundering, and sentenced in March 2024 to 25 years. Ellison, Gary Wang and Nishad Singh pleaded guilty and testified.

What makes it a documented conspiracy rather than a failure

Intent was established at trial. The prosecution's case rested on insiders who described knowingly moving customer money, and on documents and code showing the mechanism.

The audit trail exists. Unlike a rumour, this is a case where the ledger, the chat logs and the testimony are all available to courts. The estate has been recovering assets and distributing them to creditors.

It is not evidence that crypto is inherently fraudulent — a distinction the coverage often blurred. It is evidence that an unregulated custodian with weak controls can lose customer assets, which is an old story with new vocabulary.

The lesson for reading claims

The warning signs were public: an exchange also running a proprietary trading firm, a token whose value supported the balance sheet, a founder treated as a genius by the press, and no meaningful audit.

That is a checklist, not hindsight. It is the same structure this archive applies to proven plots: follow the custody of the money, and ask who could verify the claim and did not.
