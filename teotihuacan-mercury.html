Mercury Under Teotihuacan

Liquid mercury was found in a tunnel under the Feathered Serpent pyramid. It is documented, and it is not evidence of anything exotic.

The find

In 2003 a tunnel was found beneath the Feathered Serpent pyramid at Teotihuacan, sealed around 1,800 years ago. Excavation from 2009 revealed offerings, pyrite mirrors and, in 2015, liquid mercury in a chamber.

Maps, diagrams and images

The interpretation

Mercury occurs naturally and was used in Mesoamerican ritual; its presence is interpreted as a representation of an underworld river or as a reflective surface. The find is documented in the excavation reports, and the interpretation is archaeology rather than anomaly.

The archive files it as documented because the material and its context are published.

What is established

Documented: the tunnel and the offerings.

Documented: the liquid mercury.

Documented: Mesoamerican use of mercury.

Interpreted: as a ritual representation.

The label: documented.

Why it is in the archive

Because it is the kind of find that generates headlines and has a plain archaeological reading.
