Ashley Madison's Bots

A dating site whose user base included large numbers of fabricated female profiles. The data leak made it measurable.

The site

Ashley Madison marketed itself for extramarital affairs. After a 2015 data breach, analysis of the leaked records indicated that a very large share of the female profiles were inactive or fabricated, and that the site had used automated accounts to engage male users.

Maps, diagrams and images

The consequences

The company settled with the US regulator over deceptive practices, and the episode is documented in security research and reporting. It became the standard example of a platform whose apparent population was partly synthetic.

The archive files it as documented.
