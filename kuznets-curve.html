The Kuznets Curve

Inequality rises with development and then falls. The data behind it were thin and the pattern is not general.

The claim

Simon Kuznets proposed that inequality rises during early industrialisation and falls later, based on limited data from a few countries. The 'environmental Kuznets curve' applies the same shape to pollution.

Maps, diagrams and images

The evidence

Later data show no consistent inverted-U across countries; some grow with rising inequality, others with falling. The environmental version holds for some pollutants and not others, and the shapes are documented as case-specific.

Where it stands

Documented: the proposal.

Documented: the thin original data.

Not general: the pattern.

The honest label: live debate.

Why it is in the archive

Because it is a curve drawn through six points and quoted for seventy years.
