Dark Photons

A hypothetical particle that would interact weakly with ordinary matter, proposed as a bridge to a dark sector.

The idea

A dark photon would be a particle like the photon but with mass, mixing weakly with ordinary electromagnetism. It is proposed as a mediator for dark matter and as a solution to several anomalies.

The searches

Experiments use laser interferometry, resonant cavities and beam-dump setups to look for the mixing. None has found a signal, and the allowed parameter space is being narrowed. The candidate remains viable in some ranges.

The archive files it as live debate: a well-motivated candidate with no detection.

Where it stands

Documented: the theoretical motivation.

Documented: the experiments.

Not detected: any signal.

Narrowing: the parameter space.

The honest label: live debate.

Why it is in the archive

Because it is the kind of candidate that keeps physics interesting: cheap to propose, hard to find.
