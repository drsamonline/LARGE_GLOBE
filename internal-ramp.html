The Internal Ramp

A proposal that the Great Pyramid was built using an internal spiral ramp rather than external ones. The evidence is argued.

The proposal

A French team proposed that a spiral ramp inside the pyramid, using the Grand Gallery as part of the system, moved blocks into place, with a counterweight mechanism. The proposal explains some features and requires others to be reinterpreted.

Maps, diagrams and images

The evidence

The internal structure is documented by survey and by muon imaging, which found a large void above the Grand Gallery in 2017. Whether the void is a construction ramp is not established, and other explanations — external ramps, sledges and levers — remain live.

Where it stands
