Trickle-Down Economics

A nickname for supply-side tax cuts, and a claim about who benefits. The evidence is contested and the term is a slogan.

The claim

'Trickle-down' describes the argument that tax cuts for high earners and businesses raise investment and growth, benefiting everyone. It is used more as a label by critics than by advocates, who prefer 'supply-side'.

Maps, diagrams and images

The evidence

Studies of tax cuts find effects on growth that are small and disputed, and documented increases in inequality after major cuts. The mechanism by which gains would spread is argued rather than demonstrated, and the term itself is a rhetorical device.

Where it stands
