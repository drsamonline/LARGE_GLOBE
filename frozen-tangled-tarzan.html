Frozen, Tangled and Tarzan

The theory that Tarzan is Anna and Elsa's brother, shipwrecked as a baby. It rests on a ship, a timeline and a set of parents who look similar.

The theory

The theory holds that the King and Queen of Arendelle, who leave in Frozen and are lost at sea, survived long enough to have a child who became Tarzan — making the three characters siblings. Supporters point to the ship in the shipwreck sequence, the parents' appearance and the timelines.

Maps, diagrams and images

The problems

The films are separate franchises, made by different creative teams, and the timelines do not align: Tarzan is set in the late nineteenth century and Frozen in a fictional Scandinavia. The resemblance of the parents is a matter of animation style.

The theory was popular enough that it has been discussed by people who worked on the films, generally as an entertaining coincidence.

What is established

Documented: the theory and the details cited.

Not established: any shared continuity.

Documented: the separate productions and timelines.

The weakness: the timeline.

The label: fiction.

Why it is in the archive

Because it is the best-known Disney crossover theory, and because the timeline is the detail that settles it.
