Relational Quantum Mechanics

The proposal that quantum states are always relative to an observer, and that there is no observer-independent description.

The interpretation

Carlo Rovelli proposed that a quantum state is not absolute but relational: it describes a system relative to another system. Different observers can assign different states to the same system, and both can be correct.

Maps, diagrams and images

The debate

The interpretation resolves some measurement puzzles and raises questions about consistency between observers, which later work has addressed with frameworks for agreement. It makes the same predictions as standard quantum mechanics.
