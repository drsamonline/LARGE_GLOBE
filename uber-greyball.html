Uber's Greyball

Software that showed a different version of the app to regulators and to people the company wanted to avoid.

The tool

Greyball was an Uber tool that identified users likely to be regulators or opponents, using data about their devices and behaviour, and showed them a different version of the app — including fake cars that never arrived — so that enforcement operations were hampered.

Maps, diagrams and images

The exposure

The practice was reported in 2017 and confirmed in an internal investigation commissioned by the company, which published a summary. Regulators investigated, and the tool was discontinued.

The archive files it as documented.
