Stevie Wonder Can See

A claim that a blind musician is not blind. It is a joke that has been repeated as fact.

The claim

The claim holds that Stevie Wonder, blind since shortly after birth, can in fact see, and that his blindness is a performance. It circulates as a joke and occasionally as a sincere assertion.

Maps, diagrams and images

The facts

Wonder was born premature and blinded by retinopathy of prematurity, which is documented in his biography and in his own account. The claim rests on clips of him moving confidently, which is what a person does after decades in familiar spaces.

The archive files it as debunked, and notes that the claim is a disability denial dressed as a joke.

What is established

Documented: the medical history.

Documented: his own account.

Not established: any sight.

The mechanism: familiarity with spaces.

The label: debunked.

Why it is in the archive

Because it is a claim about a real person that a search settles, and because it is repeated anyway.
