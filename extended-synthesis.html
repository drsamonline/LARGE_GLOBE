The Extended Synthesis

The proposal that evolutionary theory needs more than the modern synthesis: development, plasticity, inheritance beyond genes.

The proposal

The extended evolutionary synthesis argues that evolutionary theory should give more weight to developmental bias, phenotypic plasticity, niche construction and non-genetic inheritance, rather than treating them as peripheral to the gene-centred modern synthesis.

Maps, diagrams and images

The debate

Critics argue that these phenomena are already accommodated within the existing framework and that the proposed synthesis adds vocabulary rather than mechanism. Supporters argue that the weighting changes predictions. The disagreement is documented in the literature and is partly about framing.

Where it stands

Documented: the phenomena.

Documented: the debate.

Contested: whether a new synthesis is needed.

Partly: a dispute about framing.

The honest label: live debate.

Why it is in the archive

Because it is a live argument inside a science that outsiders think is settled.
