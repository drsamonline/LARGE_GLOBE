Hair Comparison

FBI examiners testified that hairs 'matched' a suspect. The testimony overstated what the method could do.

The practice

Forensic examiners compared hairs found at crime scenes with samples from suspects, using microscopy, and testified in hundreds of trials that the hairs were consistent with a particular person or excluded others.

Maps, diagrams and images

The review

A 2015 FBI review of cases with hair testimony found that examiners had overstated the significance of matches in the large majority of cases reviewed, and that errors contributed to convictions. Microscopic hair comparison is now treated as a screening tool at most, with DNA as the identification method.
