The Hittites

An empire mentioned in the Bible, recovered from a Turkish mound with an archive of cuneiform tablets.

The recovery

The Hittite capital Hattusa, near modern Boğazkale in Turkey, was excavated from 1906, revealing a royal archive of thousands of cuneiform tablets in Hittite and other languages. The empire had been known from Egyptian and biblical references and was recovered as a documented state.

Maps, diagrams and images

What the archive shows

The tablets include treaties, laws, letters and ritual texts, including a peace treaty with Egypt after Kadesh. The language was deciphered in the early twentieth century, and it proved to be Indo-European — an unexpected result that reshaped the map of the family.

The archive files it as documented because the tablets and the decipherment are published.
