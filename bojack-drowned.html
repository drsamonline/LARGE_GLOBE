BoJack Drowned

A theory that the horse died in a swimming pool and the rest of the series is his dying imagination. It is a reading of a show about a character who nearly dies.

The theory

The theory holds that BoJack Horseman died in the pool at the end of the third season and that the remaining seasons are his dying fantasy. Supporters cite the show's dream sequences, its use of repetition and its preoccupation with death.

Maps, diagrams and images

The problems

The series continues with events that are clearly presented as real, including a trial and a prison sentence, and its creators have described the ending as real. The theory treats an ambiguous style as a hidden plot, which is the same move as the 'coma' theories elsewhere in this archive.

The archive files it as fiction and notes the method: ambiguity read as revelation.

What is established

Documented: the theory and the scenes cited.

Documented: the show's later plot.

Not established: the death.

The method: ambiguity as revelation.

The label: fiction.
