Key. Documented primary records or peer review · Live debate serious people disagree · Fringe claim no evidence offered · Debunked tested and failed. A claim can be interesting and still be filed as debunked — that is the point of the register.

I · Space, physics and realityThe ones that start with an equation
ClaimLabelWhere and why
Many-worlds interpretationDocumentedEverett, 1957. Mainstream physics; an interpretation, not a proven fact — no experiment yet separates it from rivals.
Simulation hypothesisLive debateBostrom's trilemma. A rigorous argument that does not establish its third horn.
Quantum immortalityLive debateDerived from many-worlds; requires dropping the Born rule and a "you" that crosses branches.
One-electron universeDebunkedWheeler to Feynman, 1940. Abandoned by both; no account of particle number.
Boltzmann brainsLive debateA working reductio in cosmology, used against eternal-fluctuation models.
Dark forest theoryLive debateLiu Cixin, 2008. A fiction premise with two stipulated axioms.
Zoo hypothesisLive debateBall, Icarus, 1973. In the dark forest dossier; same untestability, opposite motive.
Black Knight satelliteDebunkedThe 1998 photograph shows a thermal blanket lost on a spacewalk.
Inner sun / dual sunsFringe claimFrom hollow-Earth literature; no observation supports a second local star.
Grandfather paradoxLive debateA real problem for closed timelike curves; Novikov's self-consistency is the main answer.
White holesLive debateThe time-reversed black-hole solution; never observed, and the Big Bang is not one.
Project Blue BeamFringe claimIn the UFO dossier: a hologram apocalypse that absorbs every future observation.
II · Mind, philosophy and natureThe ones that start with a thought
ClaimLabelWhere and why
Last ThursdayismFringe claimUnfalsifiable by construction — the page argues that is the lesson, not the claim.
SolipsismFringe claimIn the Last Thursdayism dossier; a serious position that cannot be lived or tested.
The EggDocumentedAndy Weir, 2009 — documented as fiction, accurately credited, and older than him as an idea.
Roko's BasiliskFringe claimA Pascal's mugging that spreads by warning about itself.
Electromagnetic soulFringe claimReal fields, real measurements, and a leap the numbers cannot carry.
Bicameral mindLive debateJaynes, 1976. Influential; no neurological mechanism, and ancient texts contradict it.
Stoned ape hypothesisFringe claimReal pharmacology, real brain-size change, no evidence linking them.
The BloopDebunkedIn Underwater Worlds; NOAA attributes it to icequakes, and it was never in the Mariana Trench.
Mass psychogenic illnessDocumentedStrasbourg 1518, Tanganyika 1962, Salem — with the records.
False memory (Mandela effect)Live debateNamed 2009; explained by memory research rather than timelines.
Gaia hypothesisLive debateStrong version rejected (no unit of selection); weak version is now Earth system science.
III · History and geographyThe ones that start with a document
ClaimLabelWhere and why
Phantom time (614–911 AD)DebunkedTree rings, eclipses, Chinese annals and coins all close the gap.
Hollow EarthDebunkedSeismology, gravity and boreholes; the full dossier.
Expanding EarthDebunkedLost its mechanism to plate tectonics; the page explains why it faded.
Continental driftDocumentedWegener 1912, ridiculed, then vindicated — the archive's founding example.
Lemuria, Mu, Kumari KandamLive debateInvented continents vs real drowned land: Zealandia, Sundaland, Doggerland.
Tartaria / mud floodDebunkedBuilding styles, census records and photographs; needs evidence destroyed to survive.
Giant skeletonsDebunkedCardiff Giant and the rest; the photographs are the evidence against them.
Ancient astronautsDebunkedReal monuments, invented mechanism, no prediction.
Silurian hypothesisDocumentedFrank & Schmidt, 2018 — published, peer-reviewed, and explicitly not a claim that one existed.
IV · Internet lore and pop cultureThe ones that start with a post
ClaimLabelWhere and why
Dead internet theoryLive debateBots are ~half of traffic; "most readers are bots" does not follow.
The BackroomsDocumentedOne 2019 photograph and one paragraph — openly fiction, and better for it.
The Wyoming IncidentDebunkedNo FCC record, no station log; contrast the real Max Headroom hijacking of 1987.
PolybiusDebunkedFirst appears around 2000; no cabinet, no photograph, no operator.
Pixar TheoryDocumentedNegroni, 2013 — labelled as fan criticism, and the Easter eggs are real.
Rugrats theoryDebunkedReinterpretation with nothing behind it; authorship points the other way.
Paul is deadDebunkedNo crash, no funeral, no gap in a documented public life.
The 27 ClubDebunkedA list, not a distribution: age 27 is where these deaths peak anyway.
Mummy's curseDebunkedTested as a cohort study in the BMJ, 2002: no effect.
Amityville, Hope DiamondDebunkedSPR files it as a hoax; the Smithsonian dates the diamond's curse to 1908.
Creepypasta and Slender ManDocumentedFiction with a documented origin — and documented real-world harm in 2014.
SpongeBob seven sinsDebunkedSeven characters, seven sins — some mapping always fits. Never confirmed by the writers.
Ed, Edd n Eddy purgatoryDebunkedSkin tones and absent adults are animation conventions, not evidence of death.
V · Claims that turned out to be trueThe ones where the paranoia was right
ClaimLabelWhere and why
MKUltraDocumentedCIA, 1953–73; exposed by the Church Committee and FOIA.
Tuskegee syphilis studyDocumented1932–72; penicillin withheld; exposed in 1972.
Operation MockingbirdDocumentedChurch Committee and Bernstein, 1977.
Operation NorthwoodsDocumented — proposed, rejectedSigned proposal, declassified 1997, never executed. "They discussed it" ≠ "they did it".
Nayirah testimonyDocumentedFabricated incubator testimony, arranged through a PR firm.
The Business PlotDocumentedButler's testimony, McCormack–Dickstein report, no prosecutions.
WatergateDocumentedThe benchmark: documents and tapes outran the cover-up.
COINTELPRO, Tonkin, Iran-Contra, tobaccoDocumentedFour more, each with the document that proved it.
Bohemian GroveDocumentedA real club in a real forest. Real ≠ shadow government.
Germ theory / SemmelweisDocumentedVindicated after his death — and a case of consensus inertia, not conspiracy.
Hope Diamond provenanceDocumentedA real object with a real paper trail — and a manufactured curse.
Operation PaperclipDocumentedOver 1,600 German scientists recruited; Nazi records sanitised. Confirmed in government files.
