Sterile Neutrinos

A hypothetical neutrino that interacts only through gravity. Several anomalies pointed to it; the experiments have not confirmed it.

The candidate

A sterile neutrino would mix with the known neutrinos and interact only gravitationally. It was proposed to explain anomalies in short-baseline experiments and as a dark matter candidate.

Maps, diagrams and images

The searches

Dedicated experiments — MiniBooNE, MicroBooNE and others — have tested the anomaly. MicroBooNE's results did not confirm the simplest sterile-neutrino explanation, and the parameter space has narrowed, though the anomalies are not fully resolved.
