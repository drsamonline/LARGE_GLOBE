Linking the Fire

The choice at the end of a game about cycles. It is a reading of a fiction, and the fiction is about reading.

The choice

In Dark Souls the player chooses whether to link the fire and continue the cycle or let it fade. The series is explicit that the cycle is a trap, and the choice has been read as a comment on sequels and repetition.

Maps, diagrams and images

The status

The reading is an interpretation of a work of fiction and is supported by the games' own text. Filed as fiction.

What is established

Documented: the game and its ending.

Documented: the reading.

Not a claim: about the world.

The label: fiction.

Why it is in the archive

Because it is a game about cycles that is itself inside one.
