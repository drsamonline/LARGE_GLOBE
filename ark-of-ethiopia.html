The Ark in Ethiopia

A church in Aksum claims to hold the Ark of the Covenant. The claim is a matter of faith, and no examination has been permitted.

The claim

The Ethiopian Orthodox Church holds that the Ark of the Covenant is kept in the Chapel of the Tablet at Aksum, and that it was brought there in antiquity. The claim is part of the church's tradition and is defended on theological grounds.

Maps, diagrams and images

The problems

Only one guardian is permitted to see the object, and no independent examination has been allowed. There is no archaeological or documentary evidence for the transfer, and the biblical Ark's own history ends with the destruction of the First Temple. The claim is not testable as things stand.

The archive files it as fringe, and notes that it is a religious claim rather than a hoax.

Where it stands

Documented: the tradition and its antiquity.

Not examined: the object.

No evidence: for the transfer.

Not testable: under current rules.

The honest label: fringe.

Why it is in the archive

Because it is a claim that could be settled in an afternoon and is protected from being settled.
