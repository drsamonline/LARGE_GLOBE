Cantre'r Gwaelod

A drowned kingdom under Cardigan Bay, kept out by a gate that was left open. The legend is real; the kingdom is not.

The legend

Cantre'r Gwaelod is a legendary drowned kingdom in Cardigan Bay, lost when a gatekeeper failed to close the sea wall. It appears in Welsh poetry and prose from the medieval period onwards.

Maps, diagrams and images

The evidence

There is no archaeological evidence of a drowned kingdom, though the bay has submerged forests and peat beds from post-glacial sea level rise — real features that the legend may draw on. The story is documented as literature.

Where it stands

Documented: the legend.

Documented: submerged forests in the bay.

Not found: any kingdom.

Literary: the source.

The honest label: fiction.

Why it is in the archive

Because it is a legend with a real drowned landscape behind it, which is the honest version of Atlantis.
