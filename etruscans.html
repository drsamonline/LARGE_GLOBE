The Etruscans

The civilisation that taught Rome its rituals, and whose language is still only partly read. Their origin was argued for centuries and settled by DNA.

The civilisation

The Etruscans dominated central Italy before Rome, with a league of cities, elaborate tombs, and a religion whose rites Rome absorbed. Their language is written in an alphabet derived from Greek and is only partly understood.

Maps, diagrams and images

The origin question

Herodotus said they came from Lydia; Dionysius said they were indigenous. A 2021 study of ancient DNA from Etruscan individuals found them genetically similar to their Italian neighbours, supporting a local origin with a distinctive culture rather than a migration.

The archive files it as documented because the DNA result and the archaeology agree.

What is established

Documented: the cities, tombs and inscriptions.

Documented: Roman borrowing of Etruscan rites.

Documented: a local origin, by DNA.

Partly understood: the language.

The label: documented.

Why it is in the archive

Because it is a case where genetics closed a question the ancient sources could not.
