Phoebe Is Homeless

The joke theory that Phoebe in Friends is homeless and sleeps in the coffee shop. It is contradicted by the show, and it is funny.

The theory

The theory observes that Phoebe is rarely shown at home, spends her time in Central Perk, has an unusual living situation, and behaves in ways the others find strange — and concludes that she is homeless and sleeping in the coffee shop.

The problems

The show depicts her apartment, her roommates and her address across its run. The theory is a joke built on selective observation, and it belongs to the genre of 'dark readings of sitcoms'.

The archive keeps it because the method — selecting the scenes that fit — is the same one used on much more serious pages.

What is established

Documented: the theory and the scenes cited.

Documented: the show's depiction of her home.

Not established: any homelessness.

The method: selective observation.

The label: fiction.

Why it is in the archive

Because it is the funniest version of the archive's central error, and because it is honest about being a joke.
