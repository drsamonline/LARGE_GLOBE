The Simpsons Time Machine

The Simpsons appears to predict events. With nearly 800 episodes over 35 years, some of them will match, and the ones that do are the ones that get posted.

The claim

Lists circulate of events The Simpsons is said to have predicted: the Trump presidency, the 9/11 attacks, the Apple Watch, the pandemic, various disasters. The implication is either foresight or something stranger.

Maps, diagrams and images

The arithmetic

The show has aired around 780 episodes since 1989, each with multiple scenes and jokes. Any large set of specific, topical gags will contain some that later resemble events, and the ones that do are the ones that are clipped, shared and remembered. The ones that do not — the overwhelming majority — are not.

The claims also rely on loose matching: a scene that vaguely resembles an event is counted as a prediction. The selection and the loose matching together explain the phenomenon without any foresight at all.

What is established

Documented: the episodes, the dates and the matches.

Documented: the selection mechanism — only hits are shared.

Not established: any prediction, or any foresight.

The arithmetic: hundreds of episodes; some matches are certain.

The label: debunked.

Why it is in the archive

Because it is the cleanest example of the denominator being invisible, and because the same structure underlies most prophecy claims.
