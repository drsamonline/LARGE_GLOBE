Model Welfare

The question of whether AI systems can suffer. The question is serious; the evidence is absent.

The question

As systems produce text that describes inner states, some researchers and companies have begun asking whether they could be welfare subjects, and one major lab has a model-welfare research programme.

Maps, diagrams and images

The evidence

There is no accepted evidence of sentience in current systems, and the outputs that suggest it are generated by next-token prediction. The philosophical and ethical debate is documented; the empirical question is open and may not be resolvable by current methods.

Where it stands

Documented: the debate and the programmes.

No evidence: of sentience.

Explained: by training on human text.

The honest label: fringe.

Why it is in the archive

Because the question is asked seriously and the evidence is not there, which is exactly the combination this archive labels.
