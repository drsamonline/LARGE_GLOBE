The Phantom Cosmonaut Tapes

Recordings said to capture dying Soviet cosmonauts. The recordings exist; the interpretation has never been verified.

The tapes

The Judica-Cordiglia brothers, Italian amateur radio enthusiasts, recorded transmissions during the 1960s that they said came from Soviet spacecraft in distress, including what appeared to be a woman's voice and a failing heartbeat. The recordings circulated widely and are the basis of the 'lost cosmonaut' claims.

Maps, diagrams and images

The status

The tapes exist. Their content has never been verified: the signals cannot be traced to a specific spacecraft, the voices do not match known personnel, and the Soviet archives, opened after 1989, record no concealed crews. The brothers were sincere enthusiasts, and their recordings may include real transmissions that have been misidentified.

The archive files it as live debate because the artefact is real and the interpretation is not settled.

Where it stands

Why it is in the archive

Because it is the artefact behind the lost-cosmonaut story, and because the artefact and the story have different evidential status.
