Zero and Its Transmission

The numeral system with place value and zero travelled from India to the world. The route is documented.

The system

The place-value decimal system with a zero symbol is documented in Indian mathematics from the early centuries AD, and appears in inscriptions. It was adopted in the Islamic world, where it was described by al-Khwarizmi, and reached Europe through Latin translations.

Maps, diagrams and images

The details

The transmission route is documented, though the specific channels and the date of the zero's first use are argued. The European name 'Arabic numerals' reflects the route rather than the origin, which is a documented misnomer.

What is established

Documented: the Indian origin.

Documented: the transmission.

Argued: the channels and dates.

The label: documented.

Why it is in the archive

Because it is the most consequential export in the history of science, and its route is on paper.
