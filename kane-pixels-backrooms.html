Kane Pixels' Backrooms

A YouTube series that turned an imageboard joke into a film. It is fiction, made by a named creator, and it has a studio deal.

The series

In 2022 a creator posting as Kane Pixels released a short film set in the Backrooms — the endless office spaces invented on an imageboard in 2019 — using found-footage techniques and practical effects. The series continued and attracted a large audience.

Maps, diagrams and images

The status

The series is fiction, made by a named creator who has discussed it, and it led to a feature-film deal with a studio. The original Backrooms post is also fiction, and the archive treats both as such.

It is the clearest case of internet fiction becoming conventional film without any claim of reality.

What is established

Documented: the series, its creator and its reception.

Documented: the studio deal.

Not a claim: about the world.

The path: imageboard fiction to film.

The label: fiction.

Why it is in the archive

Because it shows the genre's route into the mainstream, and because nobody involved pretended otherwise.
