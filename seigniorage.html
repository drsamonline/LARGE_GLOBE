Seigniorage

The profit from issuing money, and the reason governments can fund themselves by printing.

The concept

Seigniorage is the revenue a government obtains from issuing currency — the difference between the face value of money and the cost of producing it, plus the purchasing power gained by creating reserves.

Maps, diagrams and images

The limits

The mechanism is documented and is how hyperinflations happen: as the public reduces holdings of a depreciating currency, the revenue from printing falls and the temptation to print more rises. The relationship is documented in the hyperinflation literature.
