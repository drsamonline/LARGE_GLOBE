MyHouse.wad

A Doom level released with a story about a friend who died. It is a work of art in a 1993 game engine, and it is fiction.

The work

MyHouse.wad is a modification for the 1993 game Doom, released in 2023, presented with a document describing a friend who made the level before his death. The level itself is a house that changes as the player moves through it, using the engine's limits as part of the effect.

Maps, diagrams and images

The status

The work is fiction, made by a known modder, and the framing document is part of the fiction. Its reception as a serious piece of design is documented in games writing.

The archive files it as fiction and notes the technique: a very old engine used to produce something new.

What is established

Documented: the work and its author.

Documented: the framing document as fiction.

Documented: its critical reception.

Not a claim: about the world.

The label: fiction.

Why it is in the archive

Because it is the best recent example of fiction that uses a format's limitations as its subject.
