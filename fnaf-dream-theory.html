The FNAF Dream Theory

The theory that the whole game series is a child's dream. It was popular, it was abandoned by the community, and the creator denied it.

The theory

The 'dream theory' held that the events of the Five Nights at Freddy's series take place in the mind of a child, with the animatronics and the murders as dream material. It was popular in 2015 and was argued from details across the games.

Maps, diagrams and images

The problems

The series' creator addressed the theory and the later games contradicted it, establishing a history, a timeline and a cast of characters. The fandom moved on, and the theory is now treated as an early phase of interpretation.

The archive files it as fiction and notes that a fandom can retire a theory without the work doing so.

What is established

Documented: the theory and its arguments.

Documented: the later games' continuity.

Contradicted: by subsequent releases.

Status: abandoned by the community.

The label: fiction.

Why it is in the archive

Because it is a case of a theory being tested by new evidence — which is rare in fandom.
