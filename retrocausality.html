Retrocausality

The proposal that later events can influence earlier ones. Some interpretations use it; no experiment has shown it.

The proposal

Retrocausal interpretations of quantum mechanics allow future measurement choices to constrain past states, in order to explain entanglement without action at a distance. Transactional and two-state-vector formalisms use the idea.

Maps, diagrams and images

The status

The interpretations reproduce quantum predictions and do not permit signalling into the past, so the retrocausality is not observable as such. Experiments designed to test it have found results consistent with standard quantum mechanics.

Where it stands

Documented: the interpretations.

Same predictions: as standard QM.

No signalling: into the past.

Not demonstrated: as a physical effect.

The honest label: live debate.

Why it is in the archive

Because it is the interpretation that sounds most impossible and is mathematically respectable.
