The Twitter Files

Internal documents released by a new owner, describing moderation decisions. Their content and their framing are both documented.

The documents

In 2022 the new owner of Twitter gave selected journalists access to internal documents about content moderation, including the handling of a news story about a laptop and decisions about accounts. The documents were published in a series of threads and articles.

The assessment

The documents show that moderation involved judgement calls and some errors, and they do not support claims of a coordinated government censorship programme. Analyses by journalists and researchers have documented both the contents and the selective framing of the release.

What is established

Documented: the documents.

Documented: the moderation decisions described.

Documented: the errors found.

Not established: a censorship programme.

The label: documented.

Why it is in the archive

Because it is a case where the primary documents are public and the story told about them is not the same thing.
