Cattle Mutilations

Cattle found dead with clean cuts and missing organs, said to be the work of something unusual. The forensic explanations are ordinary and varied.

The phenomenon

From the 1960s, ranchers in the United States reported cattle found dead with the eyes, ears, tongue or reproductive organs removed, clean-edged wounds, and no blood at the site. The reports peaked in the 1970s and were attributed variously to cults, government agencies, predators and UFOs.

The explanations

Forensic work has found that scavengers — coyotes, birds, insects — produce wounds with clean edges, and that the removal of soft tissues follows a predictable sequence. Bloating and decomposition can cause the hide to split and the body to appear drained. Some cases have involved human interference, and a few have been prosecuted.

No single explanation covers every case, and none requires anything exotic.

Where it stands

Documented: the deaths and the wound patterns.

Documented: scavenger and decomposition effects.

Documented: some human interference.

Not established: any exotic cause.

The honest label: live debate about individual cases.

Why it is in the archive

Because it is a case where the forensic answer is known and the stories outlived it.
