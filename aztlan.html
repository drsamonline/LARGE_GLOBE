Aztlán

The ancestral homeland of the Mexica, described in colonial sources and located nowhere.

The tradition

Aztlán is described in colonial-era sources as the homeland from which the Mexica migrated to the Valley of Mexico. The accounts were written after the conquest, from oral tradition and pictographic records.

Maps, diagrams and images

The location

Proposals place it in northern Mexico, the southwestern United States or further north; none is established. The name has also been adopted as a cultural and political symbol, which is a separate matter from the historical question.

Where it stands

Documented: the migration tradition.

Documented: the colonial sources.

Not established: any location.

Symbolic: use in modern politics.

The honest label: fringe.

Why it is in the archive

Because it is a real tradition with no site, and the absence is the finding.
