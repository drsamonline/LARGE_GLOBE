The Dungeons & Dragons Panic

A game blamed for suicides and Satanism. The evidence was never there, and the panic is documented.

The panic

From the late 1970s, claims circulated that Dungeons & Dragons led players into Satanism, suicide and violence, driven by a missing-player case and by advocacy groups. Schools and churches banned it, and hearings followed.

Maps, diagrams and images

The evidence

No study found the claimed effects, and the panic is documented as a moral panic in media-studies literature. The game's players were surveyed repeatedly without showing elevated harm, and the episode is now a standard case study.

What is established

Documented: the panic and its claims.

Not found: the claimed harms.

The label: debunked.

Why it is in the archive

Because it is the template for every subsequent panic about a new medium.
