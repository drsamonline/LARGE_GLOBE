Andrew Carlssin

A story about a man who turned $800 into $350 million and claimed to be a time traveller. It ran in a supermarket tabloid.

The story

In 2003 a story circulated that a man named Andrew Carlssin had been arrested by the SEC after turning $800 into $350 million in short order, and had told investigators he was a time traveller from 200 years in the future. It was published by the Weekly World News.

Maps, diagrams and images

The facts

The Weekly World News was a satirical tabloid, and no such case, arrest or person exists in any record. The story has been repeated by outlets that did not check its source, which is how it became a fixture.

The archive files it as debunked because the origin is a single satirical publication.

What is established

Documented: the story and its source.

Documented: the publication's satirical nature.

Not established: any such person or case.

Propagated by: unchecked repetition.

The label: debunked.

Why it is in the archive

Because it shows how a joke in one publication becomes a case file everywhere else.
