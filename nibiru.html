Nibiru

A claimed planet on a long orbit that would pass Earth. It has been predicted repeatedly and has never appeared.

The claim

Nibiru was popularised as a planet on a 3,600-year orbit whose passage would cause catastrophes. Dates for its arrival were given for 2003, 2012 and later, and none produced anything.

Maps, diagrams and images

The evidence

No such planet has been detected by any survey, and a body of that mass on that orbit would be among the brightest objects in the sky. The claim's astronomical content has been rejected by astronomers, and its dates have passed without event.

What is established

Documented: the claim and its dates.

Documented: the sky surveys.

Not detected: any such planet.

Passed: the predicted dates.

The label: debunked.

Why it is in the archive

Because it is the doomsday claim with the clearest test, and the test was run.
