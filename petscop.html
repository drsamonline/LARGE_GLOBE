Petscop

A YouTube series presenting footage of a nonexistent 1990s PlayStation game. The game was never made, and the story is in the editing.

The series

Petscop began in 2017 as a series of videos showing gameplay from a supposed unreleased PlayStation game, in which a character catches 'pets' in a strange, empty world. The footage is accompanied by a narrative about a family, a disappearance and a hidden second game.

Maps, diagrams and images

The status

The game does not exist. The series was made by a creator who has discussed it, and its puzzles, codes and edits are the work. It is fiction presented in the format of a playthrough, which is the same technique as found footage applied to games.

The archive files it as fiction, and notes the technique because several pages here treat internet artefacts as evidence.

What is established

Documented: the series and its dates.

Documented: the game's nonexistence.

Not a claim: about the world.

The technique: a fictional playthrough.

The label: fiction.

Why it is in the archive

Because it is the best-made example of a fictional artefact that looks like a discovered one.
