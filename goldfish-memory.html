The Goldfish Memory Myth

The claim that fish forget everything in three seconds. Experiments show learning that lasts months.

The claim

The claim that goldfish have a three-second memory is repeated in advertising and conversation. It has no research basis.

Maps, diagrams and images

The evidence

Experiments have shown that goldfish learn to associate cues with food and retain the association for months, and that they can be trained to perform tasks. The three-second figure has no source.

What is established

Documented: learning and retention.

Documented: training studies.

Not sourced: the three seconds.

The label: debunked.

Why it is in the archive

Because it is a claim about an animal that a tank and a few weeks would settle.
