The Green Coffee Bean Scam

A weight-loss supplement promoted on television with a study that was retracted and a researcher who was paid.

The claim

A green coffee bean extract was promoted as a weight-loss supplement, with a study reporting substantial loss and appearances on a daytime television show. The product sold widely.

Maps, diagrams and images

The exposure

Investigations found the study had been altered, that its lead author had misrepresented the data and had been paid by the manufacturer, and the paper was retracted in 2014. The Federal Trade Commission brought cases against sellers, and the researcher was debarred.

The archive files it as documented.
