Prion-Like Spread

Misfolded proteins that propagate through the brain, implicated in several neurodegenerative diseases. The mechanism is documented for prions; its reach is argued.

The documented case

Prion diseases — Creutzfeldt–Jakob, kuru, scrapie, BSE — are caused by misfolded proteins that induce misfolding in normal copies and spread through tissue. This is established, and it overturned the assumption that infectious agents must carry nucleic acid.

Maps, diagrams and images

The extension

The proposal that tau, alpha-synuclein and amyloid-beta spread in a prion-like way through the brain is supported by pathology patterns and animal experiments, and it is not the same as being infectious between people. The extent to which this explains disease progression is debated.

Where it stands

Documented: prion diseases.

Supported: prion-like spread of other proteins.

Not infectious: in the same sense.

Contested: how much it explains.

The honest label: live debate.

Why it is in the archive

Because it is a mechanism that broke a rule and then got applied more widely than the evidence supports.
