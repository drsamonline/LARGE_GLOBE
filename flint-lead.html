Flint's Lead

A city switched its water source to save money, and the water corroded the pipes. The lead poisoning is documented.

The sequence

In 2014 Flint, Michigan, switched its water supply to the Flint River as a cost measure. The water was corrosive and was not treated with corrosion inhibitors, and lead leached from the city's pipes into drinking water.

Maps, diagrams and images

The response

Residents and researchers documented elevated blood lead levels, particularly in children; the city returned to its previous supply in 2015, and officials were prosecuted, with some convictions later overturned on procedural grounds. The pipe replacement programme is documented.

The archive files it as documented.
