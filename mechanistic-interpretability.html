Mechanistic Interpretability

The attempt to reverse-engineer what a model is computing inside. Results are documented; the general claim is not.

The field

Mechanistic interpretability tries to identify the internal circuits and features a model uses — for example, induction heads, specific feature directions, and circuits for particular tasks — and has produced documented findings in small models.

Maps, diagrams and images

The limits

Whether the approach scales to frontier models, and whether it can give assurance about behaviour, is argued. Partial explanations of small circuits are documented; a general account of a large model is not.

Where it stands

Documented: specific circuit findings.

Contested: scaling to large models.

Not established: assurance.

The honest label: live debate.

Why it is in the archive

Because it is the field that would make AI claims checkable, and it is not there yet.
