E.T. and Star Wars

A cameo put E.T.'s species in the Star Wars senate, and a fan theory built a shared universe on it. The cameo is real; the universe is a joke.

The cameo

In Star Wars: Episode I – The Phantom Menace (1999), a scene in the Galactic Senate includes a brief appearance by a species that resembles E.T. — a deliberate nod by George Lucas, whose company had worked with Spielberg. The resemblance is documented and was intended as an in-joke.

Maps, diagrams and images

The theory

The theory extends the joke into a shared universe: that E.T. is a Star Wars alien, and that his people are part of the Republic. It is a fan construction, not a plot in either franchise, and the filmmakers have described the cameo as an Easter egg.

The archive files it as fiction and notes the cameo, because the cameo is the only real part.

What is established

Documented: the cameo and its intent.

Documented: the theory.

Not established: any shared universe.

The mechanism: an Easter egg read as canon.

The label: fiction.

Why it is in the archive

Because it is a case where the smallest possible piece of evidence is real and the conclusion is not.
