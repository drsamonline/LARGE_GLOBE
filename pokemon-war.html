The Pokémon War

A single line about a war, and a theory that Kanto was devastated by one. The line exists; the war does not.

The theory

In the original games, Lieutenant Surge mentions that he used electric Pokémon during a war, and the theory expands this into a full backstory: a conflict that devastated Kanto, killed many adults, and explains why so many children are travelling alone and why the region has so few middle-aged men.

Maps, diagrams and images

The problems

The line is a character detail, of the kind that gives a gym leader a past. The rest of the theory is inference from the games' design — sparse populations, young protagonists — which is a consequence of the hardware and the genre, not of a war.

It is an unusually coherent fan theory and it remains a theory.

What is established

Documented: the line about the war.

Documented: the theory and its arguments.

Not established: any war in the setting.

The weakness: design constraints read as history.

The label: fiction.

Why it is in the archive

Because it is the best example of a theory built from a game's technical limitations read as worldbuilding.
