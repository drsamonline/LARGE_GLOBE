Technosignature Pollution

The idea that a civilisation could be detected by its pollution — nitrogen dioxide, chlorofluorocarbons, industrial waste — rather than by its radio.

The idea

Technosignatures are detectable signs of technology. Besides radio and megastructures, proposals include atmospheric pollutants: nitrogen dioxide from combustion, chlorofluorocarbons from refrigeration, and industrial aerosols, all of which have spectral features that could be seen in an exoplanet atmosphere.

Maps, diagrams and images

The status

The idea has been modelled and searches have been proposed, with current instruments too insensitive for most of the predicted signals. No technosignature has been detected, and the approach's value is that it broadens the search beyond radio.

The archive files it as live debate.
