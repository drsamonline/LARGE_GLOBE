Seven themes, one shelf

Every dossier belongs to exactly one theme. The themes are not genres — they are groups of claims that share the same kind of evidence, which is why reading them together teaches you more than reading them apart. An ice wall and a hollow earth are both claims about the inside of the planet; a simulation and a Boltzmann brain are both claims about the odds of being a mind.

The same grouping drives the homepage cards and the tags at the top of every dossier, so wherever you start, the shelf around you is visible.
