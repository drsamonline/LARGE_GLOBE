NESARA and GESARA

A claimed law that would cancel all debt, abolish income tax and deliver prosperity packages. It has been 'about to be announced' for thirty years.

The claim

NESARA — the National Economic Security and Reformation Act — is said to be legislation passed in secret in 2000 that would abolish income tax, cancel personal debt, return to a gold standard and distribute 'prosperity packages'. GESARA is the global version. The story spread through channelled messages, internet radio and, later, political conspiracy channels.

Maps, diagrams and images

The record

No such law exists in any public record. The original source was a Nevada-based figure who claimed to have been contacted by ascended masters; the act was never introduced, let alone passed. Announcement dates have come and gone for decades, and the packages have never been delivered.

The structure — a secret law that will imminently transform the world, blocked by a hidden cabal — is the same as the 'imminent disclosure' pattern in UFO lore.

What is established

Documented: the claim, its sources and its history.

Documented: the absence of any such legislation.

Documented: the repeated failed announcement dates.

Not established: any of it.

The honest label: debunked.

Why it is in the archive

Because it is the purest example of the 'imminent' claim, and because its thirty-year record of non-arrival is the evidence.
