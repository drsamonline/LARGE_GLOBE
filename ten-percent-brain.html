The Ten Per Cent Brain

The claim that we use a tenth of our brains. Imaging shows activity throughout.

The claim

The claim that humans use only ten per cent of their brains is repeated in advertising and self-help, and has been attributed to Einstein among others without evidence.

Maps, diagrams and images

The evidence

Imaging studies show metabolic activity across the brain over a day, and damage to almost any region produces deficits — which would not happen if the region were unused. The figure's origin is traced to popular writing rather than research.

What is established

Documented: the claim.

Documented: whole-brain activity.

Documented: deficits after injury.

The label: debunked.

Why it is in the archive

Because it is the most successful false fact about the brain ever told.
