Deepwater Horizon Cement

A well blowout that killed eleven people and spilled millions of barrels. The cement job and the decisions around it are documented.

The well

In April 2010 the Macondo well in the Gulf of Mexico blew out. The cement barrier at the bottom failed, gas reached the rig, and the blowout preventer did not seal. Eleven workers died, and oil flowed for months.

Maps, diagrams and images

The findings

The presidential commission and other investigations documented a series of failures: cement design, negative pressure test interpretation, and management decisions under time pressure. The findings are published, and the litigation is on the record.

The archive files it as documented.
