The Taylor Swift Clone

A claim that a celebrity has been replaced by a double. It is the oldest rumour in fandom, applied to a new subject.

The claim

The claim holds that the singer has been replaced by a lookalike, based on photographs in which she looks different. It is a variant of the 'Paul is dead' and 'Avril replaced' rumours.

Maps, diagrams and images

The problems

Photographs differ with lighting, age, makeup and angle; the claim offers no evidence beyond that, and no discontinuity in her public record. The pattern — a famous person replaced by a double — recurs across fandom and has never been supported in any case.

The archive files it as debunked.

What is established

Documented: the claim and its variants.

Documented: the photographs cited.

Not established: any replacement.

The pattern: older than the subject.

The label: debunked.

Why it is in the archive

Because it is the same rumour every generation, and the archive can show the repetition.
