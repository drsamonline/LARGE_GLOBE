Gadget Is a Decoy

The theory that Inspector Gadget is a decoy while his niece does the work. It is a joke about the show's structure, and the show's structure supports it.

The theory

The theory observes that Inspector Gadget is incompetent, that his gadgets misfire, and that Penny and Brain solve every case — and concludes that Gadget is a deliberate decoy, a walking distraction maintained by the agency while his niece does the actual work.

Maps, diagrams and images

The response

The show's formula does put Penny at the centre of every solution, and the theory is a neat reading of it. It is not the creators' stated intention: the premise is a parody of gadget-driven spy fiction, in which the gadgets fail and the child succeeds.

The archive files it as fiction because the reading is an interpretation of a formula, not a hidden plot.

What is established

Documented: the show's formula.

Documented: the theory and its arguments.

Not established: any intended decoy plot.

The reading: an interpretation of structure.

The label: fiction.

Why it is in the archive

Because it is a case where the fan reading and the creators' intention are both true at different levels.
