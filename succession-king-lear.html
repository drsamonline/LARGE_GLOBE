Succession as King Lear

The comparison between the Roy family and Shakespeare's tragedy is made constantly. It is a useful reading and it is not a hidden source.

The comparison

Critics have compared Succession to King Lear since its first season: an ageing patriarch dividing an empire among children who cannot inherit it well, with flattery, betrayal and a fool. The comparison is a critical commonplace rather than a claim about the writers' sources.

Maps, diagrams and images

The problems

The show's creators have cited other influences, and the parallels are structural — a genre pattern as old as tragedy. Treating the comparison as a claim of adaptation would be a mistake; treating it as a reading is standard criticism.

The archive files it as fiction because the comparison is interpretation, not evidence of a hidden text.

What is established

Documented: the comparison in criticism.

Documented: the structural parallels.

Not established: adaptation or hidden source.

The status: a reading.

The label: fiction.

Why it is in the archive

Because it is a case where the comparison is the point and nobody is claiming otherwise.
