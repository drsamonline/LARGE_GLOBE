Aryabhata's Rotation

A fifth-century astronomer stated that the Earth rotates. The text is real and the claim is in it.

The text

The Aryabhatiya, composed in 499, includes a statement that the apparent motion of the stars is caused by the Earth's rotation, and gives astronomical computations of considerable accuracy for their time.

Maps, diagrams and images

The caveats

The text is documented and the statement is there. Claims that Aryabhata anticipated Copernicus in a full heliocentric system are not supported: his system is geocentric with a rotating Earth, and the later tradition did not adopt the rotation.

What is established

Documented: the text and the statement.

Documented: its computations.

Not supported: a heliocentric system.

The label: documented.

Why it is in the archive

Because the real achievement is impressive and the exaggerated version is unnecessary.
