How Modern Humans Spread

Out of Africa, with interbreeding and multiple dispersals. The broad picture is settled and the details are not.

The outline

Modern humans originated in Africa and dispersed across the world, interbreeding with Neanderthals and Denisovans along the way. The genetic and archaeological evidence for this is strong.

Maps, diagrams and images

The arguments

The number and timing of dispersals, the routes through Arabia and Asia, and the status of early sites in Australia and the Americas are all argued. Ancient DNA has repeatedly revised the picture, usually by adding complexity.

Where it stands

Documented: the African origin.

Documented: interbreeding.

Contested: the routes and the timing.

Revised: repeatedly by DNA.

The honest label: live debate.

Why it is in the archive

Because it is the best-evidenced story about us, and the evidence keeps changing the story.
