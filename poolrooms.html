The Poolrooms

An endless complex of tiled pools and corridors, invented on an imageboard. It is a liminal-space fiction, and it is labelled as one.

The fiction

The Poolrooms appeared in 2022 as an imageboard post: an image of an infinite tiled pool complex with no exits, accompanied by a short description. It joined the Backrooms as one of the internet's liminal spaces — places that are empty, familiar and wrong.

Maps, diagrams and images

The status

The Poolrooms is fiction, created and circulated as such, and it has no claim to be anything else. Its influence on video games and horror writing is documented.

The archive files it as fiction because that is what it says it is — the same category as the Backrooms and SCP.

What is established

Documented: the origin and the aesthetic.

Documented: its influence.

Not a claim: about the world.

The genre: liminal-space fiction.

The label: fiction.

Why it is in the archive

Because it is a clean example of a fiction that never pretends otherwise, which makes it a useful contrast.
