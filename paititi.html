Paititi

A lost Inca city said to lie east of the Andes. Expeditions have searched; nothing has been found.

The legend

Paititi is a legendary Inca refuge said to lie in the cloud forest east of Cusco, sometimes described as a city of gold. It appears in colonial-era accounts and in centuries of exploration stories.

Maps, diagrams and images

The evidence

No site matching the description has been found. Archaeology has documented Inca and pre-Inca settlements in the eastern slopes, and lidar surveys have revealed substantial earthworks, but none is Paititi. The legend's details shift between sources.

Where it stands

Documented: the legend and its history.

Documented: Inca settlement in the region.

Not found: any city of gold.

Shifting: the descriptions.

The honest label: fringe.

Why it is in the archive

Because it is El Dorado's highland cousin, and the archaeology is more interesting than the legend.
