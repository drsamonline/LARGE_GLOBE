The Mushroom War

Adventure Time is set a thousand years after a nuclear war, and the show reveals it slowly. The backstory is documented in the episodes, not invented by fans.

The setting

Adventure Time is set in the Land of Ooo, a world of mutants, magic and ruins. The series establishes over its run that Ooo is Earth after a nuclear war — the 'Mushroom War' — around a thousand years earlier, and that the show's magic is a consequence of the aftermath.

Maps, diagrams and images

The reveal

The backstory is delivered through artefacts, flashbacks and the histories of characters such as the Ice King and Marceline, and it is confirmed in later episodes and in the finale. It is not a fan construction: the show's own writers built the setting on it.

The archive files it as documented because the claim is about what the text contains, and the text contains it.

What is established

Documented: the war, the timeline and the reveal.

Documented: the episodes that establish it.

Not a fan theory: the writers built the setting on it.

Fiction: the setting itself.

The label: documented, as a reading of the show.

Why it is in the archive

Because it is a case where a fan observation turned out to be the intended reading, which is rarer than the reverse.
