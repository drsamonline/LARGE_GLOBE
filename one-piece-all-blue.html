One Piece and the All Blue

A theory about where a legendary sea is. It is an interpretation of a long-running manga.

The theory

The theory holds that the All Blue — a sea said to contain fish from every ocean — will be created or revealed by events at the end of the story, rather than existing undiscovered.

Maps, diagrams and images

The status

The manga has not concluded, so the question is open within the fiction. Filed as fiction, because it is a prediction about a story.

What is established

Documented: the work and the legend within it.

Documented: the theory.

The label: fiction.

Why it is in the archive

Because it is a prediction about a story rather than about the world, and the difference matters.
