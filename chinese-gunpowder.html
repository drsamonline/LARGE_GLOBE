Chinese Gunpowder Weapons

Gunpowder was formulated in China for medicine and fireworks, and by the Song dynasty it was a weapon.

The development

Gunpowder appears in Chinese texts from the ninth century, first in alchemical and medical contexts, then in weapons: fire arrows, bombs and eventually cannon. Song dynasty sources describe defences against siege using explosive weapons.

Maps, diagrams and images

The transmission

The technology spread west along trade routes, reaching the Islamic world and Europe by the thirteenth century, where its metallurgy and chemistry developed further. The Chinese origin is documented in texts and in the archaeological remains of bombs.

The archive files it as documented because the sources and the finds agree.
