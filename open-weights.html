Open Weights

Models released for anyone to run. The benefits and the risks are both documented, and the argument is live.

The practice

Open-weight models are published with their parameters downloadable, so they can be run and modified locally. Several capable families are released this way, and the practice is documented.

Maps, diagrams and images

The argument

Documented benefits include competition, inspection, local control and research access. Documented risks include the removal of safety filters and use for fraud or bioweapon research. Both sides cite evidence, and the net assessment is contested.

Where it stands

Documented: the releases.

Documented: benefits and risks.

Contested: the net effect.

The honest label: live debate.

Why it is in the archive

Because it is the open-source argument again, with higher stakes and better documentation.
