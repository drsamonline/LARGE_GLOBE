QBism

An interpretation in which the quantum state is a person's expectation, not a fact about the world.

The interpretation

Quantum Bayesianism treats the quantum state as an agent's personal probability assignment rather than a description of reality, and the Born rule as a guide to action. Measurement outcomes are real and personal.

Maps, diagrams and images

The debate

The interpretation dissolves some puzzles and has been criticised for making physics subjective and for struggling to explain agreement between observers. It makes no different predictions from standard quantum mechanics, so the choice is interpretive.

Where it stands

Documented: the interpretation.

Same predictions: as standard QM.

Contested: its account of objectivity.

Interpretive: not empirical.

The honest label: live debate.

Why it is in the archive

Because it is the interpretation that takes the observer seriously, and the cost is the point.
