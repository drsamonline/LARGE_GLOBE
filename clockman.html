Clockman

A short animation remembered by a viewer and identified years later. It is a lost-media success, and the memory turned out to be accurate.

The case

A viewer remembered a short animation from childhood in which a clock-headed figure frightened children, broadcast on a children's channel. The memory was vague, and a search community formed to identify it.

Maps, diagrams and images

The outcome

The short was identified as a segment from a European animated production, and the identification was confirmed with the creators and with surviving copies. It is one of the clearer successes of the lost-media search community.

The archive files it as documented because the object exists and was found.

What is established

Documented: the memory and the search.

Documented: the identification and its confirmation.

Documented: the surviving copies.

Not a claim: about anything but the archive of television.

The label: documented.

Why it is in the archive

Because it is the counter-example to Saki Sanobashi: a search that found the thing.
