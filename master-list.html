The Master List

Everything on the list, in one place: a verdict, a reason in a sentence, and a link where a full dossier exists. Entries with no link to a dossier are catalogued but not yet written up — that is a to-do, not a verdict.

Key: DOC documented · DEB live debate · FRI fringe, no evidence · DEB'D tested and failed. A verdict on a claim is not a verdict on the people who believe it.

How to read this page

The verdict is about the claim, not the person. A fringe label means no evidence has been offered that could settle it, not that the believer is stupid. A debunked label means the specific claim was tested and failed — usually after being taken seriously by someone qualified.

Entries with no link have no dossier yet. That is the archive's honest state: 150 claims catalogued, 51 written up in depth. The register at Every Claim is the same content in shorter form.
