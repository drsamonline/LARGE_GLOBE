Black Mirror's One Timeline

A theory that every Black Mirror episode shares a universe, connected by background details. The details are real; the continuity is not.

The theory

The theory holds that the episodes of Black Mirror take place in one timeline, with recurring technologies and news items linking them. Supporters cite background details: the same fictional company, the same headlines, the same devices.

The problems

The show's creator has described the connections as Easter eggs rather than continuity, and several episodes present mutually incompatible futures. The details are real and the timeline is an invention.

The archive files it as fiction and notes the method, which is the same one used for the Pixar and Marvel theories.

What is established

Documented: the recurring details.

Documented: the creator's description.

Not established: a single timeline.

Incompatible: some episodes' futures.

The label: fiction.

Why it is in the archive

Because it is a clean example of Easter eggs being read as continuity.
