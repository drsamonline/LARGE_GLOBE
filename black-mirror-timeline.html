Black Mirror's One Timeline

A theory that every episode shares a single timeline. It is an interpretation of an anthology series.

The theory

The theory collects Easter eggs across episodes — company names, news clips, songs — and argues that the series depicts one future history rather than unconnected stories.

Maps, diagrams and images

The status

The series' creator has described the references as Easter eggs rather than a continuity, and the anthology format works against a single timeline. Filed as fiction.

What is established

Documented: the references.

Documented: the creator's comments.

Not canon: a shared timeline.

The label: fiction.

Why it is in the archive

Because it is the most popular continuity theory in television, and the creator answered it.
