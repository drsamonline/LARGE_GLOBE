The Upside Down Time-Freeze

A theory that the other dimension in Stranger Things is frozen in 1983. It is an interpretation of a work of fiction.

The theory

The theory holds that the Upside Down is stuck at the moment Will was taken in 1983, which is why its version of Hawkins is unchanged, and that later events in the show occur in a copy rather than the original.

Maps, diagrams and images

The status

The reading is consistent with some details and contradicted by others; the show's creators have discussed the dimension without confirming it. It is an interpretation, and the archive files it as fiction.

What is established

Documented: the show and its details.

Documented: the theory's reasoning.

Not canon: unless confirmed.

The label: fiction.

Why it is in the archive

Because it is a good reading of a text, which is a different thing from a claim about the world.
