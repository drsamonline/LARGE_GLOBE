Emergent Gravity

The proposal that gravity is not fundamental but arises from deeper physics, as temperature arises from atoms.

The proposal

Several programmes propose that gravity is emergent: thermodynamic (Jacobson, Padmanabhan, Verlinde), holographic, or entropic. On these views spacetime and gravity are collective descriptions of underlying degrees of freedom.

Maps, diagrams and images

The status

The approaches reproduce some results and have not produced a complete theory or a distinctive prediction that has been confirmed. The 2016 galaxy-scale prediction of Verlinde's version has not been supported by data.

Where it stands

Documented: the programmes.

Reproduced: some results.

Not complete: as a theory.

Not supported: by galaxy-scale tests.

The honest label: live debate.

Why it is in the archive

Because it is the most attractive idea in gravity and it has not delivered a test.
