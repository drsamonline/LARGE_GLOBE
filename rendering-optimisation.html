Rendering Optimisation

The claim that reality only renders what is observed, as a game engine does. The observation is real; the engine is a metaphor.

The claim

The claim holds that the universe behaves like a rendering engine that only computes what is being observed, saving resources — and that quantum measurement is the render.

Maps, diagrams and images

The evidence

Quantum measurement does not work like a render-on-demand system: interference is observed in unmeasured systems, and the mathematics of quantum mechanics is a continuous description. The metaphor is useful for explaining superposition and misleading as a mechanism.

Where it stands

Documented: the metaphor's use.

Documented: quantum measurement.

Not supported: the engine reading.

Metaphor: not mechanism.

The honest label: fringe.

Why it is in the archive

Because it is the most common way people explain quantum mechanics to themselves, and it is wrong in an interesting way.
