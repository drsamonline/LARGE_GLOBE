Ezekiel's Wheel

A vision of wheels within wheels, read by some as a description of a spacecraft. The text is a prophetic vision.

The text

The first chapter of Ezekiel describes a vision of four living creatures, wheels with eyes and a throne of fire, in the context of the Babylonian exile. It is a prophetic and priestly text.

Maps, diagrams and images

The reading

From the 1960s, writers including Erich von Däniken read the vision as an eyewitness account of a landing spacecraft. The reading requires treating a visionary text as reportage and ignoring the literary conventions of apocalyptic literature.

Why it is in the archive

Because it is the founding text of the ancient-astronaut reading of scripture.
